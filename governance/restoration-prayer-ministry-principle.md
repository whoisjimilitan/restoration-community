# GOV-002: Restoration Prayer Ministry Principle

## Purpose

Define the spiritual and ministry principles governing prayer within Restoration Community.

This document establishes that **prayer is not a platform feature but the spiritual foundation underneath the entire restoration journey.**

Prayer determines the character of everything built after it: mentoring, safeguarding, notifications, testimony, community, administration, and participant experience.

## Part 1: Spiritual Foundation

### Prayer is Foundational, Not Optional

Prayer is not an activity added to Restoration Community.

**Prayer is the foundation through which participants seek God throughout their restoration journey.**

The Restoration Journey consists of seven stages:
- Truth
- Confession
- Repentance
- Forgiveness
- Reconciliation
- Honest Work
- Serving Others

At every stage, participants encounter God through prayer.

Truth requires prayer: "Lord, give me courage to face reality."

Confession requires prayer: "Lord, give me humility and honesty."

Repentance requires prayer: "Lord, give me strength to turn away."

Forgiveness requires prayer: "Lord, give me freedom and healing."

Reconciliation requires prayer: "Lord, restore relationships."

Honest Work requires prayer: "Lord, establish integrity and provision."

Serving Others requires prayer: "Lord, make my testimony and service useful for Your purpose."

### Jesus Christ is the Source of Restoration

Restoration does not come from:
- The platform
- Mentors
- Community
- Self-effort
- Programs

**Restoration comes from Jesus Christ.**

Prayer is the spiritual channel through which participants connect with God's restoring work.

The platform and ministry exist to support this connection, not replace it.

### Participants Seek God Through Prayer

A participant comes to Restoration Community because:
- Their life has been shaped by deception, fraud, addiction, or hidden sin
- They recognize they need restoration
- They are willing to pursue honest work and living
- They seek God's intervention, guidance, and healing

**Prayer is how they seek.**

The platform makes it possible.

The ministry facilitates it.

**But God does the restoration.**

## Part 2: Prayer Ministry Leadership

### Brother Jimi's Role

Brother Jimi serves Restoration Community as:
- **Servant of God** — His first allegiance is to Jesus Christ
- **Teacher** — He teaches the restoration journey and principles of restoration
- **Mentor** — He walks alongside participants in their restoration
- **Shepherd** — He provides pastoral care and spiritual oversight
- **Prayer Leader** — He leads the prayer ministry and stands in prayer for participants

**Brother Jimi is the spiritual covering and authority for prayer ministry.**

### Prayer Ministry Structure

The prayer ministry operates under Brother Jimi's spiritual leadership.

**Prayer Warriors** serve as an extension of the ministry. They are:
- Selected and equipped by Brother Jimi
- Sent to pray for specific participants or needs
- Accountable to Brother Jimi for faithfulness and confidentiality
- Servants, not judges; intercessors, not counselors

**The internal structure of prayer ministry remains invisible to participants** unless circumstances require transparency (e.g., a specific mentor praying for a participant).

Participants experience:
- **The face of the ministry is Brother Jimi**
- There is a servant of Jesus called Brother Jimi who prays for repentant ex-scammers
- They can reach out to this prayer ministry
- They will be prayed for

Participants do NOT experience:
- Prayer team rosters
- Prayer queue systems
- Internal routing logistics
- Organizational complexity
- Multiple access points or departments

**Simplicity in participant experience. Complexity handled invisibly.**

## Part 3: Prayer Request Flow

### The Flow

```
Participant
    ↓
Prayer Request
(submitted to Brother Jimi's prayer ministry)
    ↓
Brother Jimi Prayer Ministry
(receives and considers the request)
    ↓
Prayer Warriors
(assigned when appropriate by Brother Jimi's direction)
    ↓
Prayer (intercession for participant)
    ↓
Prayer Follow-up
(God's response, continued ministry contact)
```

### Participant Experience

A participant seeking prayer:
1. Reaches out to Brother Jimi's prayer ministry (via simple submission)
2. Submits their prayer request (confidential form)
3. Receives acknowledgment that prayer will be offered
4. Experiences prayer ministry intercession (privately, between participant and God)
5. Receives follow-up and pastoral care

### Ministry Experience

The prayer ministry:
1. Receives prayer requests
2. Prays and discerns how to respond
3. Assigns prayer warriors as appropriate
4. Follows up with participants
5. Records ministry actions (for accountability and remembrance of God's work)
6. Maintains confidentiality and pastoral responsibility

### What Prayer Requests Are NOT

Prayer requests are **not primarily community posts.**

Prayer requests are **not social broadcasts.**

Prayer requests are **not discussion forums.**

Prayer requests are **not ways to get sympathy or public attention.**

Prayer requests are **petitions brought before God through the ministry of prayer.**

## Part 4: Confidentiality Principle

### Where Vulnerability Belongs

A person's vulnerability belongs first between:
- **The person and God**
- **The person and trusted servants involved in ministry**

Not:
- The person and the community
- The person and public platforms
- The person and unvetted individuals

### Privacy as Default

**Prayer requests are private by default.**

A participant coming from:
- Fraud or deception
- Shame or hidden sin
- Addiction or compulsion
- Broken relationships
- Fear or trauma
- Hidden struggles

...will not expose their deepest vulnerability publicly.

**The platform provides a safe door:**
- Private submission
- Confidential handling
- Ministry prayer
- God's response
- Then, if the participant chooses, testimony

### Pastoral Responsibility

The prayer ministry handles requests with:
- **Wisdom** — discerning how to respond appropriately
- **Respect** — honoring the participant's dignity and circumstances
- **Confidentiality** — protecting what has been entrusted
- **Care** — maintaining pastoral relationship with the participant

**Safeguarding and pastoral responsibility guide the handling of all sensitive matters.**

Some requests may involve:
- Legal proceedings that require confidentiality
- Family circumstances that require discretion
- Mental health or trauma that requires specialized care
- Ongoing situations that require long-term ministry attention

The prayer ministry determines appropriate response based on pastoral judgment, not automatic sharing.

## Part 5: Testimony Principle

### What Testimony Is

Testimony is:
- **Evidence of God's faithfulness** — It shows what God has done
- **Part of restoration** — It completes the journey from brokenness to wholeness
- **Encouragement to others** — It strengthens faith and hope in those who hear it
- **A way participants serve others** — Testimony becomes service; it saves souls

### Testimony Glorifies God

Testimony declares:
> "This is what God has done in my life. I was broken. God has restored me. Jesus is real. Restoration is possible."

Testimony does not declare:
> "Look at how much I have accomplished. Look at how strong I am. Look at my success."

**Testimony belongs to God, not the participant.**

### Spiritual Growth Toward Testimony

Participants are **discipled toward testimony as spiritual growth.**

This means:
- The ministry encourages participants to see their restoration as God's work
- The ministry teaches that God's work deserves to be made known
- The ministry prays that participants will gain courage and freedom to speak
- The ministry walks with participants toward openness about what God has done

**This is spiritual formation, not pressure.**

### Testimony and Readiness

**Public testimony is not forced disclosure.**

Some testimonies are meant to be shared publicly:
- They glorify God
- They strengthen the community
- They invite others into restoration
- The participant has freedom and desire to share

Some testimonies must remain within ministry leadership:
- The participant is not yet ready
- Family circumstances require discretion
- Legal proceedings require confidentiality
- Safeguarding concerns require protection
- The testimony involves others who have not consented
- The sharing would retraumatize the participant

### The Principle

> **"God's work should not be hidden when it can bring Him glory and help others."**

This is not:
> "Everyone must publicly reveal everything about their restoration."

This is:
> "When God works, and when sharing brings glory to God and hope to others, the ministry creates space and courage for testimony. When sharing would harm or violate, the ministry respects readiness and circumstances."

### Integration With Serving Others

**Serving Others (Stage 7) is inseparable from testimony.**

As participants mature in restoration, they serve by:
- Sharing what God has done
- Walking alongside those just beginning
- Testifying to God's faithfulness
- Inviting others into the restoration journey
- Becoming witnesses that restoration is real

Testimony becomes the most powerful form of service.

## Part 6: Prayer Throughout the Restoration Journey

### Every Stage is Surrounded by Prayer

The seven stages of restoration are not self-directed programs to complete.

**They are spiritual transitions to be lived in prayer.**

#### Stage 1: Truth
> **Prayer:** "Lord, give me courage to face reality. Help me see my life as it truly is. Free me from denial and deception."
>
> Prayer at this stage asks for honest sight, unflinching reality, courage to acknowledge what has been hidden.

#### Stage 2: Confession
> **Prayer:** "Lord, give me humility and honesty. Help me speak what I have hidden. Free me from shame so I can confess."
>
> Prayer at this stage asks for the humility to acknowledge sin without excuse, the honesty to speak truth, the freedom to name what has been concealed.

#### Stage 3: Repentance
> **Prayer:** "Lord, give me strength to turn away. Help me leave this path and walk toward You. Give me the courage to begin again."
>
> Prayer at this stage asks for power to change direction, commitment to a new way, strength for the difficulty of turning.

#### Stage 4: Forgiveness
> **Prayer:** "Lord, free my heart. Forgive me as I accept Your forgiveness. Help me release the guilt I carry and receive Your grace."
>
> Prayer at this stage asks for deep healing, freedom from condemnation, internalization of God's mercy, restoration of peace.

#### Stage 5: Reconciliation
> **Prayer:** "Lord, restore relationships. Guide me in pursuing peace where I have caused harm. Heal what I have broken."
>
> Prayer at this stage asks for wisdom in restoration, courage to face those we've hurt, grace to rebuild trust, peace in relationships.

#### Stage 6: Honest Work
> **Prayer:** "Lord, establish the work of my hands. Give me integrity in provision. Help me build a life founded on truth."
>
> Prayer at this stage asks for provision, dignity, success in honest work, freedom from desperation that leads back to deception.

#### Stage 7: Serving Others
> **Prayer:** "Lord, make my life useful for Your purpose. Help my testimony save others. Guide me to serve as I have been served."
>
> Prayer at this stage asks for purpose, boldness in testimony, wisdom in mentoring, continued transformation as a servant.

### Prayer as Continuous Presence

Prayer is not a stage to pass through.

**Prayer is the continuous spiritual breath throughout the entire journey.**

Participants return to prayer again and again:
- When they struggle
- When they need strength
- When they experience breakthrough
- When God speaks
- When they witness restoration in others
- When they become servants themselves

## Part 7: Implementation Boundaries

### What This Governance Does NOT Define

This document establishes **spiritual and ministry principles**, not technical specifications.

This document does **not** authorize or encourage:

#### ❌ Prayer Metrics
No counting, measuring, or ranking of prayers.

"How many prayers answered" is not a success metric.

Prayer is not a performance to be quantified.

#### ❌ Prayer Rankings
No comparison of participants by prayer frequency or fervor.

"This person prayed X times" does not indicate spiritual maturity.

Prayer is between a person and God, not a leaderboard.

#### ❌ Gamification
No badges for prayer.

No streaks for consistent prayer.

No levels based on prayer participation.

Prayer is not a game.

#### ❌ Public Prayer Wall as Default
Prayer requests are private by default.

A public prayer board turns vulnerability into spectacle.

The default experience is confidential connection to ministry, not community announcement.

#### ❌ Replacement of Pastoral Ministry With Software
The platform enables prayer ministry.

The platform does **not replace** Brother Jimi's pastoral judgment, prayer warriors' intercession, or the Holy Spirit's work.

Technology is servant, not master.

#### ❌ Automated Spiritual Decisions by the Platform
The platform does not decide:
- Who gets prayer
- Whether prayer is "appropriate"
- Whether a testimony should be shared
- Who can access prayer requests
- How to respond to requests

**These are pastoral decisions made by ministry, not algorithmic decisions made by software.**
