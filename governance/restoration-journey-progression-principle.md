# GOV-001: Restoration Journey Progression Principle

## Purpose

Define the authoritative ministry understanding of how participants progress through the seven-stage Restoration Journey.

This governance establishes the meaning, mechanism, and roles involved in moving from one restoration stage to the next.

This document is binding authority for all platform implementations, including JOURNEY-002 and beyond.

## What Progression Means

### The Core Definition

**Progression through the Restoration Journey represents continued faithful participation in a way of life.**

Progression is NOT:
- Achievement or accomplishment
- Spiritual rank or advancement
- Completion of a program or course
- Demonstration of spiritual maturity

Progression IS:
- Willingness to continue walking the journey
- Engagement with the next stage's teaching and reflection
- Openness to God's ongoing work of transformation
- Participation in community and accountability

### The Restoration Journey Philosophy

The seven stages are not a ladder to climb once.

They are a way of life to continue embracing.

Participants may:
- Move forward through the stages
- Return to earlier stages when needed
- Engage with multiple stages simultaneously
- Revisit stages throughout their lives

**Returning to an earlier stage is not failure. It is faithfulness.**

## Progression Model: Hybrid Participant-Led Progression

### When Progression Happens

A participant progresses to the next stage when:

1. **The participant has engaged meaningfully with the current stage**
   - Reviewed the stage teaching
   - Considered the scriptural foundation
   - Reflected on the guidance for living

2. **The participant has completed the required reflection for the current stage**
   - Submitted a reflection response
   - Engaged honestly with the reflection prompt
   - Expressed their engagement with the stage content

3. **The participant intentionally chooses to continue**
   - Indicates readiness to move to the next stage
   - Explicitly expresses willingness to progress
   - Takes action to advance (no automatic progression)

### Why This Model

This model preserves three core principles:

**Personal Responsibility**
> "Every participant remains responsible for their own choices." — Book One

Participants own their journey. Progression is not something done to them or for them. They choose to continue.

**Community Support (Not Control)**
> "No one is expected to walk it alone. Mentors walk alongside participants." — Book One

Mentors and community support the journey but do not gate-keep progression. Advancement is not conditional on permission.

**Faithful Participation (Not Achievement)**
> "Restoration grows through faithful reflection, not through simply completing tasks." — Book Three

Progression represents continued engagement with the journey, not completion of a program.

## Roles in Progression

### Participant Role

**Responsibility**
- Engage meaningfully with stage content
- Complete the required reflection
- Intentionally choose to progress
- Continue walking the journey with integrity

**Authority**
- Participants decide when they are ready to continue
- Participants are not required to wait for external approval
- Participants take the action to advance (initiative matters)

### Mentor Role

**Permission/Gatekeeping**
- ❌ Mentors do NOT approve or deny progression
- ❌ Mentors do NOT gate access to the next stage
- ❌ Mentor confirmation is NOT required

**Support & Encouragement**
- ✅ Mentors may discuss the participant's current stage
- ✅ Mentors may ask thoughtful questions about engagement
- ✅ Mentors may celebrate the decision to continue
- ✅ Mentors may encourage reflection and application
- ✅ Mentors may offer support for the journey ahead

**Definition**
> "Mentors encourage. They do not control. They invite. They do not pressure. They guide. They do not manipulate." — Book One

### Community Role

**Permission/Witnessing**
- ❌ Community does NOT approve or deny progression
- ❌ Community acknowledgement is NOT a gate
- ❌ Progression is NOT conditional on community gathering

**Celebration & Support**
- ✅ Community celebrates participant faithfulness
- ✅ Community members encourage one another
- ✅ Community prays for continued growth
- ✅ Community walks alongside each participant

**Definition**
> "Together, we celebrate progress. Together, we carry burdens. Together, we continue pursuing restoration." — Book One

### System (Platform) Role

**Recording & History**
The platform shall:
- Record when a participant progresses to the next stage
- Maintain immutable progression history
- Associate reflections with stage transitions
- Preserve the journey record for accountability and growth

**Mechanism**
The platform shall:
- Allow participants to indicate readiness when conditions are met
- Record the progression without requiring external approval
- Create a transition record containing:
  - Current participant
  - Previous stage
  - New stage
  - Timestamp of progression
  - Associated reflection
  - Reason (e.g., "participant progression")
- Display progression history as part of the participant's journey record

**NOT the system's role:**
- ❌ The system does NOT require mentor approval
- ❌ The system does NOT require community witness
- ❌ The system does NOT gate progression behind external conditions
- ❌ The system does NOT make progression automatic (participant chooses)

## Special Cases

### Returning to Earlier Stages

**Authority**
Participants may return to an earlier stage at any time.

**Reason**
> "Restoration is not always a straight path... Returning to truth is not failure. Confessing again is not failure... Continuing the journey is faithfulness." — Book Two

**System Recording**
The platform records returns to earlier stages as:
- Participant choice (not failure)
- Continued faithfulness (not regression)
- Part of the ongoing journey (not deviation)

**Mentor & Community Role**
- Mentors support the return without judgment
- Community encourages faithfulness in returning
- No shame, only encouragement toward restoration

### Multiple Simultaneous Engagements

Some participants may:
- Work primarily on current stage
- Revisit reflection from earlier stage
- Look ahead to preparation for next stage

**This is supported and encouraged.**

The journey is not rigid. The platform should enable flexible engagement.

### Participants Without Mentors

**During Phase 1**
Mentoring infrastructure (PRD 04.05) is still developing.

**Current Reality**
Not all participants will have assigned mentors immediately.

**Progression Rule**
Participants do NOT require mentors to progress.

**Support During Phase 1**
- Community gatherings provide accountability
- Reflection form provides structured engagement
- Prayer and Scripture provide spiritual guidance
- Mentor assignment happens through separate workflow

When mentors become available, they join the participant's journey but do not retroactively gate previous progression.

## Reflection as the Gating Element

### Reflection Requirement

**What triggers eligibility to progress:**
A completed reflection for the current stage.

**Why reflection (not arbitrary waiting):**
> "Restoration grows through faithful reflection, not through simply completing tasks." — Book Three

Reflection represents genuine engagement with the stage teaching.

### Reflection Characteristics

**NOT a test**
- No right or wrong answers
- No grading or scoring
- No judgment of quality

**IS a spiritual practice**
- Invitation to consider what God teaches
- Space for honest response
- Record of the participant's engagement

**Recording**
The system associates reflections with stage transitions to show:
- Participant was engaged with current stage
- Participant reflected on the teaching
- Participant made deliberate choice to continue

## Implementation for JOURNEY-002

This governance defines the rules that JOURNEY-002 will implement:

### Progression Logic

```
Current Stage Status:
  ✓ Participant is on Stage N
  ✓ Stage N reflection exists
  
Progression Condition Met:
  ✓ Participant clicks "Ready to continue"
  
System Action:
  ✓ Update UserRestoration.currentStageId to Stage N+1
  ✓ Create StageTransition record:
    - fromStageId = Stage N
    - toStageId = Stage N+1
    - transitionedAt = now()
    - transitionedById = null (participant-initiated)
    - reason = "participant progression"
  ✓ Preserve reflection history
  ✓ Display confirmation to participant
  ✓ Notify mentors/community for support (not approval)
```

### Mentor Integration (When Available)

```
After Progression:
  ✓ Mentors can see transition in participant's history
  ✓ Mentors may initiate discussion about new stage
  ✓ Mentors may celebrate the step
  ✓ Mentors may offer support for next stage
  ✓ Mentors do NOT have option to prevent/revert progression
```

### History Preservation

```
Progression Record Contains:
  ✓ Previous stage (archived, not deleted)
  ✓ New current stage (active)
  ✓ Timestamp (auditable)
  ✓ Associated reflection (context preserved)
  ✓ All transitions (immutable history)
```

## Distinction: Participant-Led vs. Automatic

### This is NOT automatic progression
- Participants must take action
- Participants must indicate readiness
- Progression requires participant choice

### This is NOT automatic gating
- Mentors cannot prevent progression
- Community does not gate progression
- Reflection is the only requirement

### This IS participant-led with support
- Participants initiate progression
- Mentors provide support (not permission)
- Community celebrates faithfulness
