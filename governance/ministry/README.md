# The Four-Book Foundation

## What is the Four-Book Foundation?

The Four-Book Foundation defines the Restoration Community as a ministry.

It establishes:
- Who we are
- How we govern ourselves
- How we disciple members
- How ministry decisions are made

The Four-Book Foundation is canonical. It is the source from which all other governance, requirements, and architecture documents derive.

## How They Relate

**Book One** (The Community Manual) answers: *Who are we?*
- The identity of the Restoration Community
- Core values and principles
- What membership and community life means

**Book Two** (The Restoration Journey) answers: *How do we grow?*
- The seven stages of restoration
- The Restoration Journey as a way of living
- How participants move through stages of transformation

**Book Three** (The Digital Community Platform) answers: *How does technology serve the community?*
- Principles governing digital community expression
- How the platform strengthens (not replaces) Christian community
- Technology's role in supporting mentoring, gatherings, and the Restoration Journey

**Book Four** (Ministry Governance) answers: *How do we operate and lead?*
- Ministry leadership and decision-making
- Resource stewardship
- Strategic direction and growth

## Authority

Documents in this folder are canonical and approved before commitment to the repository.

Changes to the Four-Book Foundation require:
1. Governance Stewards review
2. Documented rationale
3. New ADR if the change affects other systems
