# Community Covenant

## Purpose

The Restoration Community exists to walk together toward restoration through Jesus Christ.

Participation is not membership of an organisation.

It is a voluntary commitment to pursue truth, restoration, honest work, faithful relationships, and continual dependence upon Jesus Christ together with others.

This Covenant describes the spirit in which that journey is undertaken.

It is not a contract.

It is a declaration of shared commitment.

---

## The Covenant

### 1. Christ

I acknowledge that true restoration begins and ends with Jesus Christ.

No programme, mentor, community or platform can replace Him.

Everything within this community exists only to point people toward Him.

### 2. Truth

I commit myself to honesty.

I will not deliberately deceive, manipulate or misrepresent myself.

Where I fail, I will seek truth rather than hiding behind appearances.

### 3. Restoration

I understand that restoration is a journey rather than a single event.

I will embrace growth with patience.

I will not expect perfection from myself or from others.

### 4. Community

I choose to walk with others rather than alone.

I recognise that restoration flourishes within healthy relationships.

I will seek unity, encouragement and mutual care.

### 5. Humility

I acknowledge that I do not possess every answer.

I will remain teachable.

I will receive correction graciously and offer correction gently.

### 6. Compassion

Every person I encounter bears the image of God.

I will treat every participant with dignity, kindness and patience.

Even in disagreement I will refuse contempt.

### 7. Confidentiality

Where trust is given, I will protect it.

I will not disclose another person's personal journey except where required for safety or by law.

### 8. Responsibility

My restoration remains my responsibility.

The community may guide me.

Mentors may encourage me.

But no one can choose obedience on my behalf.

### 9. Service

As I grow, I will seek opportunities to strengthen others.

Restoration naturally becomes service.

Receiving eventually becomes giving.

### 10. Grace

I understand that every member of this community is imperfect.

Mistakes will occur.

Grace does not ignore truth.

Truth is always offered with grace.

### 11. Peace

I reject hostility, division and unnecessary conflict.

Where disagreement exists, I will pursue reconciliation before accusation.

### 12. Stewardship

I will use this platform honestly.

I will respect its purpose.

I will not intentionally misuse its resources or disrupt the community.

### 13. Hope

I believe restoration is possible.

Not because of human ability,

but because Jesus Christ continues to restore broken people.

That hope shapes my participation here.

---

## Acknowledgement

By continuing into the Restoration Community I affirm that:

- I have read this Covenant.
- I understand its purpose.
- I desire to participate in this spirit.
- I recognise that this Covenant guides the life of the community.
- I understand that participation remains voluntary.
- I may withdraw at any time.
