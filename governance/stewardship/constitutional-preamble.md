# The Restoration Community

## A Christ-Centred Community for People Leaving Cybercrime and Pursuing Honest Work and Living

---

# Constitutional Preamble

The Restoration Community exists to faithfully participate in God's work of restoration in Christ Jesus.

Everything we teach, organise, build, and create exists to serve that calling.

We are committed to guiding people toward honest work and living through a Christ-centred community of truth, confession, repentance, forgiveness, reconciliation, honest work, and serving others.

Jesus Christ remains the centre of our identity, our message, our community, and our hope.

The mission defines our work.

The culture protects our community.

The platform serves the mission.

Technology remains a faithful servant, never the centre.

As faithful stewards, we will protect the mission, preserve the culture, honour the dignity of every person, and remain anchored in the truth of the gospel.

Every future decision—whether in teaching, leadership, community life, or technology—must faithfully support this foundation.

By God's grace.

In Christ Jesus.

One faithful step at a time.

---

# The Four-Book Foundation

The Restoration Community is built upon four complementary books.

Each book answers a different question, yet together they form one coherent foundation.

## Book One answers:

**Who are we?**

It establishes our identity, mission, values, vision, and purpose.

---

## Book Two answers:

**How does God restore people?**

It presents the biblical Restoration Journey in Christ Jesus:

* Truth restores reality.
* Confession restores honesty.
* Repentance restores direction.
* Forgiveness restores the heart.
* Reconciliation restores relationships.
* Honest Work restores dignity.
* Serving Others restores purpose.

---

## Book Three answers:

**How do restored people live together?**

It defines the culture, relationships, leadership, mentoring, gatherings, and community life that protect and sustain restoration.

---

## Book Four answers:

**How does technology faithfully serve the mission?**

It translates the ministry into a platform that supports the Restoration Journey without ever replacing it.

---

Everything that follows should be read through this foundation.

The books are not separate visions.

They are four parts of one mission.

Jesus Christ remains the centre.

By God's grace.

In Christ Jesus.

One faithful step at a time.
