# The counsel standard (locked 22 November 2026)

The yardstick is the first three letters approved on 22 November:
"Jesus is a tomorrow thinker", "Do you want to speak? Pause a minute" and "Your future has to move into you first".
Every letter must read as well as they do, or it does not ship.

## How a letter is made from one of Jimi's messages
1. Read the whole message and name the one thing it says.
2. Choose what to use: usually one story or image, one verse, and the line that carries it.
3. Build the letter from Jimi's own sentences, trimmed and put in order. Add as few words as possible, only to join or to land a thought.
4. Let the message decide its own way in (a story, a verse, a question, a hard line), its own order and its own length.
5. If Jimi wrote a prayer or a declaration, it is the prayer. If not, a short prayer from his lines.

6. The core message is said plainly, once, in Jimi's own words, so no reader has to guess or infer what the letter means. If his post states it, that line goes in. Nothing is invented to make it clear.

7. Short sentences that land beat complete sentences that explain. A long sentence of Jimi's may be split into two, never reworded.
8. The letter's key line stands on its own. Turn to the reader once, in his words where his post has them.
9. Close on his strongest short line; the prayer echoes it.
10. Before a batch ships, Jimi reads two or three letters aloud. Wherever he stumbles, the sentence is too long.

## Fixed in every letter
- Title: a line exactly as Jimi wrote it in that message. Never shortened, rephrased or invented.
- One verse, NIV, word for word, checked against a source.
- Ends with "Today, say:" and a prayer.
- 200 words at most. Length follows the message.
- No dashes, emojis, hashtags or names of other ministers. No signature.
- The title is not repeated as the opening line.

## Never the same twice
- No two letters in a row open the same way (two questions, two stories).
- No two prayers in a week start the same way.
- No story or verse used twice in a month.

## Before a batch ships
Read the batch in a row, the way a follower scrolls the channel, beside the three yardstick letters. Rewrite anything that falls below them.
