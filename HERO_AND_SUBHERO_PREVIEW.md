## VISUAL FLOW: HERO → SUBHERO

### Hero's Job:
1. Welcome you (establish belonging)
2. Show you're understood (acknowledge your reality)
3. Offer hope (resurrection, grace, Jesus)
4. Give you a path (two CTAs: start or learn more)

### Four Pillars' Job:
1. Answer "What will I experience here?" (show platform)
2. Show it's real (concrete stages, practices, relationships)
3. Show it's integrated (pillars support each other)
4. Point to Christ (final statement)

### Together:
✓ Governance-compliant (honors Books 1-4, Engineering Charter)
✓ Trust-funnel approach (minimal, clear, actionable)
✓ Identity-communicating (shows you're entering a community, not a system)
✓ Complete on scroll (all four pillars visible on initial landing page)

- **#0F766E (Teal)** — Action, promise, hope, growth
- **#202124 (Charcoal)** — Truth, substance, reliable
