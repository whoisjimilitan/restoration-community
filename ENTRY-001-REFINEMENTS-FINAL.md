# ENTRY-001: Final Refinements
## Three Protective Measures That Keep the Ministry Christ-Centered

---

## Overview

ENTRY-001 has been refined with three critical changes that protect the ministry's integrity for years to come. These are not editorial tweaks. They are governance decisions that prevent drift.

---

## Refinement 1: Christ at the Center, Not the Ministry Leader

### What Changed

**Before:**
> "Brother Jimi's ministry remains the human doorway. The platform does not replace the servant of God."

**After:**
> "Jesus Christ is the doorway to restoration. Brother Jimi serves as the ministry's pastoral point of contact, guiding seekers toward Christ through prayer, teaching, and faithful care."

### Why This Matters

**The Risk:**
Over time, ministries can gradually drift toward treating the leader as the source of transformation. Participants say "I want to find Brother Jimi" instead of "I want to find Jesus."

This happens unintentionally—through:
- Powerful testimonies about what a mentor did
- Social media that amplifies a leader's voice
- Gamification that makes the program the hero
- Messaging that centers the community instead of Christ

**The Protection:**
By explicitly stating that **Jesus Christ is the doorway** and **Brother Jimi serves**, we establish the hierarchy once and for all.

The practical experience remains identical:
- Participants still "reach out to Brother Jimi"
- Ministry still responds personally
- Encounter still happens
- Transformation still occurs

But the governing document points seekers not to a person, but to **Jesus as the source of restoration**.

**Authority:**
Scripture and the Four-Book Foundation have always centered Christ. This refinement ensures the governance documents never drift from that foundation.

---

## Refinement 2: Pastoral Language, Not Technical Language

### What Changed

**Before:**
> "Prayer Precedes Platform Participation"

**After:**
> "Prayer is the Ministry's First Act of Welcome; Platform Participation Follows"

### Why This Matters

**The Risk:**
Technical language (platform participation, digital transformation, user pathways) can obscure what's actually happening: a broken person reaching toward God through a trusted servant.

When we use platform language for spiritual work, we accidentally treat it like a software feature instead of a mercy encounter.

**The Protection:**
Pastoral language reminds every builder, every developer, every designer: **This is ministry, not a product.**

"Prayer is the ministry's first act of welcome" creates a completely different mental model than "Prayer precedes platform participation."

One sounds like we're responding to a business inquiry.
The other sounds like we're embracing a broken person.

**Application:**
Every principle in ENTRY-001 now uses pastoral language, not technical language. This sets the tone for all dependent documents (ENCOUNTER-001, ONBOARD-002, EXPERIENCE-001).

---

## Refinement 3: New Governing Principle — The Platform Never Becomes the Hero

### What Changed

**Added as Principle 2 (new):**

> The platform must never communicate that it is the source of transformation. Jesus Christ is the source. The platform is a tool in service of that truth.
>
> Every page, feature, and message must answer: **Who is the hero of this experience?**
>
> The answer should always be: **Jesus Christ is the source. The ministry serves. The platform supports.**

### Why This Matters

**The Risk:**
Feature creep happens gradually. Each feature seems reasonable individually:
- "A prayer app to stay connected" ✓ (sounds good)
- "Prayer reminders to help you pray consistently" ✓ (helpful)
- "Track how many prayers you've prayed" ✓ (gamification)
- "See which prayers were answered" ✓ (metrics)
- "Compete on the prayer leaderboard" ✗ (drift!)

Each step from✓ to ✗ felt incremental. But together, they transformed the platform from "a tool that serves the ministry" into "a product that measures spiritual achievement."

**The Protection:**
A single, unambiguous test: **Who is the hero?**

Before each feature decision, ask:
- "Is Jesus the hero of this experience?"
- "Or have we accidentally made the platform/app/mentor/community the hero?"

If you cannot honestly say "Jesus is the hero," the feature belongs elsewhere or requires redesign.

### Examples This Prevents

❌ "Download our app and change your life" (app is hero)
❌ "Join our community and transform" (community is hero)
❌ "Our program will restore you" (program is hero)
❌ "This methodology will save you" (methodology is hero)

### Examples This Enables

✅ "Jesus restores. This ministry walks with you as you seek Him."
✅ "We pray. God works. You are transformed."
✅ "Come as you are. Christ accepts you. The journey begins with prayer."
✅ "Your vulnerability belongs to God. The ministry stands with you before Him."

### Authority

This principle is grounded in:
- Scripture (Psalm 127:1 — "Unless the Lord builds the house, those who build it labor in vain")
- Four-Book Foundation (Book Three — "The platform exists to serve the ministry, not to become the ministry")
- The lived reality of ministry transformation (God does the work; we're servants of that work)

## Refinement 4: Homepage Redesign Is Intentionally Frozen

### What Changed

**New section added:**
ENTRY-001 now **explicitly freezes** homepage redesign until three dependent authorities exist:
1. ENCOUNTER-001 — What happens after someone asks for prayer?
2. ONBOARD-002 — When and how does someone become a participant?
3. EXPERIENCE-001 — How should every page reflect this ministry pathway?

### Why This Matters

**The Problem:**
Previously, homepage redesign efforts tried to choose:
- Colors
- Typography
- Navigation structure
- Feature priorities
- Hero imagery

All without understanding **what story the website is supposed to tell**.

**The Solution:**
Now the story is clear: **A mercy encounter through which broken people discover Christ.**

But translating that story into page design requires more authority than ENTRY-001 alone provides.

**ENCOUNTER-001** will define what happens when someone requests prayer—this determines how we explain the process.

**ONBOARD-002** will define when account creation happens—this determines the information architecture and page flow.

**EXPERIENCE-001** will define how every page reflects the ministry pathway—this determines design patterns, messaging, and navigation.

Only after these three authorities exist should design begin. Then every choice will serve the story.

**Why This Is Stronger:**
- Before: Design committees making aesthetic choices based on "what looks good"
- After: Design decisions flowing from authority documents about "what the ministry does"

One is guessing. The other is implementing authority.

---

## Refinement 5: ENTRY-001 Is an Operating Model, Not Just a Design Spec

### What Clarified

Added explicit clarification that ENTRY-001 defines **why** things exist, not just **how** they work technically.

ENTRY-001 answers:
- **Why** prayer exists (first act of welcome)
- **Why** onboarding exists (transition from seeker to participant)
- **Why** the restoration journey exists (growth pathway)
- **Why** mentoring exists (pastoral support during journey)
- **Why** testimony exists (witness and multiplication)
- **Why** serving exists (continuing the cycle)

These are no longer isolated features.

They are stages in one coherent ministry movement.

### Why This Matters

**The Risk:**
Without understanding the operating model, future builders might:
- Treat features independently ("add prayer feature," "add mentoring feature")
- Optimize each feature separately (metrics for prayer, engagement scores for mentoring)
- Miss how they interconnect (testimony is the bridge from restoration to service)

**The Protection:**
By establishing ENTRY-001 as an operating model, we force all future work to ask:
- "How does this serve the complete ministry pathway?"
- "Which stage of the journey does this enable?"
- "Does this strengthen the connection from one stage to the next?"

Everything flows from the operating model, not from feature ideas.
