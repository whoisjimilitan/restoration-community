Brother JimiTo: YouThe wall you didn’t see
Today at 6:00 AM

Is there someone you still haven’t forgiven?

You might not call it unforgiveness. You’d probably say you’re just hurt, or that you were right. When you’re offended, you hardly realise you’re trapped.

Being offended isn’t what does the damage. Holding on to it is. Keep it in your heart long enough and it becomes a wall between you and God.

Jesus said this to His disciples, and it’s for you too:

“And when you stand praying, if you hold anything against anyone, forgive them, so that your Father in heaven may forgive you your sins.” Mark 11:25

So before you pray today, say their name out loud to God and tell Him you’re letting it go.

The wall between you and God comes down the moment you let go.

Your brother,
Jimi

Brother JimiTo: YouThe voice you’ll hear first
Today at 6:00 AM

When something goes wrong, whose voice do you hear first?

It’s not always the one you say you believe. It’s usually the one you’ve been listening to the most.

If you’ve been taking in the news, fear, and everyone else’s opinions, that’s what you’ll hear. Before you know it, you’re panicking, complaining, or doing things your own way.

But if you’ve been taking in God’s Word day after day, you’ll hear Him first. Instead of panicking, you’ll stop and ask, “What is God saying about this?”

This is what God told Joshua just before he led Israel into the Promised Land:

“Keep this Book of the Law always on your lips; meditate on it day and night.” Joshua 1:8

So start today. Before you check your phone, open your Bible. Take in one verse and say it out loud. Meditate on it again tonight.

Hard days will come. When they do, what you hear first will depend on what you’ve been listening to.

Your brother,
Jimi
