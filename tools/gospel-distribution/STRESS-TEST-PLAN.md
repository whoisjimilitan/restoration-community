## 1. CONTENT DISTRIBUTION — STRESS TEST

### Assumption: "Version B reels will drive discovery on social media"

**Test: What's the realistic reach?**

Given:
- VERSION B is HIDE-structured (40 sec, stops scroll)
- Posted daily to TikTok, Instagram Reels, Twitter/X
- 3 quotes per day = 3 reels per day maximum

**Conservative TikTok Performance (Day 1):**
- Uploads start at ~500–1,000 views (new account, no followers)
- Engagement rate: 2–5% (comments, shares, follows)
- Conversion to link click: 0.5–1%

**Day 1 Math:**
- 3 reels × 1,000 views = 3,000 views
- 3% engagement = 90 interactions
- 0.5% clicks = 15 people to website
- Email capture (on site): ~3 people

**Day 30 (after consistent posting):**
- Algorithm recognizes pattern (daily posting)
- Each reel: 5,000–10,000 views (growth account)
- 3 reels × 7,500 avg = 22,500 views/day
- 3% engagement = 675 interactions
- 1% clicks = 225 people to website
- Email capture: ~45 people/day

**Day 100 (momentum building):**
- Some reels go "moderate viral" (50k+ views)
- Consistent daily uploads = trusted account
- 3 reels × 15,000 avg = 45,000 views/day
- 5% engagement (higher trust) = 2,250 interactions
- 1.5% clicks = 675 people to website
- Email capture: ~135 people/day

**✓ VALIDATES** — Social drive is real and compounds

**Risk: Algorithm dependency**
- TikTok/Instagram algorithms control visibility
- Solution: Diversify — post to X (algorithm-resistant), Pinterest (discovery), YouTube Shorts (owned platform)
- Backup: Email list is owned. Revenue doesn't depend on algorithm.

**Risk: Content must be "stoppable"**
- VERSION B must truly grab attention in 3 seconds
- Solution: We test HIDE structure in VERSION B production. Jimi reviews before posting.
- Measurement: Track click-through rate weekly. If <0.5%, revise HIDE formula.
