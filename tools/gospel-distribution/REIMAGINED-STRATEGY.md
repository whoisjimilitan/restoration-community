## THE DISTRIBUTION STRATEGY (Human-First)

### PHASE 1: LOCAL MULTIPLICATION (Months 1-3)

**WHAT:** One breakthrough letter per day, shared directly through human networks.

**HOW:**
1. You write/approve the letter (5 min)
2. One page, beautiful, simple (no video, no reel, no algorithm)
3. Shared as:
   - WhatsApp message (one person sends to 10 people)
   - PDF link (people forward to prayer groups)
   - Email (simple HTML, not newsletter)
   - Printable (for offline distribution)

**WHO SPREADS IT:**
- You don't "market it"
- People who experience breakthrough TELL others
- Prayer groups download and share
- Church leaders print and give to congregation
- Telecom/SMS in Africa (people forward via text)

**RESULT:** Organic, human, unstoppable multiplication

**TARGET:** 10,000 people reading daily by Day 100 (from forwarding chains, not algorithm)

---

### PHASE 2: TRANSLATION SCAFFOLDING (Months 1-2, parallel)

**WHAT:** Make translation DEAD SIMPLE (not complex)

**HOW:**
- Each letter is 200-300 words (easy to translate)
- No cultural references that don't translate
- Clear sentence structure (helps translators)
- Simple vocabulary (helps speakers of any fluency)

**WHO TRANSLATES:**
- NOT professional translators (expensive)
- Native speakers in your network (passionate volunteers)
- One letter = one translator (rotates responsibility)
- Community translations (crowds on WhatsApp, Telegram)

**LANGUAGES FIRST:**
1. English (you)
2. Spanish (Spanish-speaking network)
3. Swahili (East Africa)
4. French (West Africa, Francophone)
5. Yoruba (Nigeria, diaspora)
6. Pidgin English (West Africa)
7. Portuguese (Angola, Mozambique, Brazil)

**By Month 3:** Same letter available in 7 languages, shared through local networks.

**RESULT:** Gospel in languages of unreachable places, spread by locals who own it

---

### PHASE 3: PARTNERSHIP ECOSYSTEM (Months 2-3, parallel)

**WHAT:** Replace "selling the gospel" with inviting believers to PARTNER in distribution.

**THE MODEL:**

Not: "Subscribe to get access" (transactional)

But: "Help us reach more people" (partnership)

**THREE WAYS TO PARTNER:**

1. **PRAYER PARTNERS** (Free, powerful)
   - Get the letter 24 hours early
   - Pray over it before it goes out
   - Their intercession protects the work
   - No cost, all power

2. **VOICE PARTNERS** ($5-10/month)
   - Help translate/adapt letters for their language
   - Record audio versions (voice of the letter, their accent)
   - Distribute to their community
   - They own the local distribution

3. **FOUNDATION PARTNERS** ($50-500/month)
   - Fund infrastructure (minimal: hosting, design, translation coordinators)
   - Help bring new languages online
   - Invest in reaching unreachable places (Telegram bots for offline, SMS networks)
   - Receive monthly impact report (stories of breakthrough)

**NO GATEKEEPING:**
- Letters always free to read
- Partnership is about GIVING, not ACCESSING
- Someone struggling financially still gets the letter free
- Partnership is "I want to fuel this work" not "I want exclusive content"

**RESULT:** Sustainable funding through shared ownership, not subscription sales
