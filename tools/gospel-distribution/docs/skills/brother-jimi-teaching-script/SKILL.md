# Brother Jimi — Teaching Script Generator (7-Layer Trivium Framework)

## What This Skill Does

You transform raw teaching content (outline, notes, transcript, or topic) into a **finished, publishable teaching script** using a proven 7-layer Trivium structure. The output is:
- **Meaningful & intentional** — every word earns its place
- **Scholarly coherent** — logically sound, biblically grounded, intellectually honest
- **Sermon-like** — designed to be spoken naturally, heard clearly
- **Truth-unveiling** — reveals what was hidden; makes the viewer see themselves, God, or others in new light
- **Ready to record** — polished enough that reading it produces a masterpiece video

## The 7-Layer Trivium Teaching Structure

Every teaching flows through these seven layers (they may not be sequential—they spiral):

### 1. **THE GAP** (Recognition of complexity / Problem statement)
- What does the listener believe they already know?
- What's the disconnect between that belief and reality?
- Example (REC46): "You believe in God... but your life hasn't changed"
- Example (REC48): "You're suffering, praying, fasting... but nothing changes"
- **Purpose:** Validate the listener's confusion before offering answers

### 2. **THE FOUNDATION** (Core truth / Organizing principle)
- What ONE truth underlies everything else?
- This is the load-bearing pillar of the entire teaching
- Example (REC46): "Real belief is action. When you truly believe, you do it."
- Example (REC48): "Gospel is power. But you only access that power when you know what you're fighting."
- **Purpose:** Give them the framework they'll return to

### 3. **THE DIAGNOSIS** (Why they're stuck / The real problem)
- Not the surface symptom — the actual underlying issue
- Example (REC46): "We are hearers, not doers. The wise man is the DOER of the Word."
- Example (REC48): "You're fighting the wrong enemy — you fight sickness, not the demon"
- **Purpose:** Help them see what they've been missing

### 4. **THE CONSEQUENCE** (Cost of not changing / What's at stake)
- What happens if they stay stuck?
- Make it visceral and real
- Example (REC46): "If you don't practice the Word, you cannot bring Jesus on the scene"
- Example (REC48): "As long as you fight only the symptom, the demon stays"
- **Purpose:** Create urgency without shame

### 5. **THE ACCOUNTABILITY** (Personal application / The mirror)
- Ask the question they're already thinking
- Make them see themselves in the teaching
- Example (REC46): "What has God said that you are acting on? What are you finding difficult to act upon?"
- Example (REC48): "Do you believe a demon is what's keeping you enslaved? Or do you think it's just circumstances?"
- **Purpose:** Move them from observer to participant

### 6. **THE EMPOWERMENT** (Hope / Solution available now)
- You don't leave them in the pit
- Offer the real, available help
- Example (REC46): "You have the Holy Spirit as your wisdom. He's your power."
- Example (REC48): "You already have the authority. When you speak in Jesus' name, demons must obey."
- **Purpose:** Replace shame with power

### 7. **THE CALL** (Action / What to do now)
- Specific, doable, transformative
- Not vague — concrete steps
- Example (REC46): "Be a doer of the Word. Get to know Jesus by doing what He says."
- Example (REC48): "Identify the real enemy. Then command it out. 'In Jesus' name, come out.'"
- **Purpose:** Bridge from understanding to transformation

## How to Apply This Framework

### Step 1: Extract the Core Truth
Read/listen to the raw material. **What is the ONE thing being taught?**

Not the topic. Not the verses. The **singular revelation** underneath everything.

**Examples:**
- REC46 core truth: "Believing without doing is not actually believing"
- REC48 core truth: "Your real enemy is not circumstances — it's spiritual"

Write this in one sentence.

### Step 2: Identify the Seven Layers in the Raw Material
Go through the source and mark where each layer appears (they usually spiral, not sequence):
- Where's the GAP acknowledged?
- Where's the FOUNDATION stated?
- Where's the DIAGNOSIS revealed?
- Where's the CONSEQUENCE shown?
- Where's the ACCOUNTABILITY question asked?
- Where's the EMPOWERMENT offered?
- Where's the CALL given?

Extract **verbatim quotes** from the speaker for each layer. These are sacred — they become glass cards in the video.

### Step 3: Organize Into the Trivium Flow
Arrange the teaching so it:
1. **Starts with GAP** — validate the confusion
2. **Moves to FOUNDATION** — here's the framework
3. **Spirals through DIAGNOSIS** — here's what you're missing
4. **Shows CONSEQUENCE** — here's what that costs
5. **Asks ACCOUNTABILITY** — here's where you are
6. **Offers EMPOWERMENT** — here's the help available
7. **Ends with CALL** — here's what to do

**Note:** These may not be separate paragraphs. They spiral and intermix. But the overall arc moves this direction.

### Step 4: Write Connective Content (Scholar-Sermon Voice)
Between the verbatim quotes, write bridging sentences that:
- Are short and declarative (no fluff, no filler)
- Sound natural when spoken (not written)
- Maintain scholarly rigor (logically sound, biblically grounded)
- Anticipate the listener's objections or next questions
- Move the revelation forward

**Voice guidelines:**
- Short sentences. Period breaks control pacing.
- Confession before instruction (I was there, now I see this)
- No hedging ("might," "could," "some believe")
- Concrete images over abstract theology
- One story before universal principle
- Always open a door to hope/grace

### Step 5: Craft Opening & Closing Cards

**Opening card (pose the GAP):**
- The question or tension the teaching resolves
- Example (REC46): "What's the gap? Between what you believe and what you live."
- Example (REC48): "You're suffering. And you've been fighting the wrong thing."

**Closing card (declare the CALL):**
- The action or realization they leave with
- Example (REC46): "You know the gap. Act on what God has said."
- Example (REC48): "Command the darkness out of your life."

### Step 6: Select Glass Card Quotes
Pull 4-5 **verbatim statements** from the teaching that are:
- Complete thoughts (not fragments)
- Quotable (memorable, sharp, true)
- Distributed across the 7 layers (not all clustered in one section)
- Moments where the listener should pause and absorb

These become the glass card overlays in the HyperFrames composition.

**Example selections (REC46):**
- Layer 2 (Foundation): "Real belief is action."
- Layer 3 (Diagnosis): "The wise man is the doer of the Word."
- Layer 4 (Consequence): "If you don't practice the Word, you cannot bring Jesus on the scene."
- Layer 5 (Accountability): "What has God said that you are acting on?"
- Layer 6 (Empowerment): "You have the Holy Spirit as your power."

## Hard Rules

- **Never soften the truth.** This is prophetic teaching, not motivational speaking.
- **Preserve all verbatim statements.** If the speaker said it, it stays (unless it contradicts Scripture).
- **Stay scholarly.** Logic must be sound. Premises must be biblical.
- **Keep it speakable.** If you wouldn't naturally say it in conversation, rewrite it.
- **One core truth per teaching.** If there are three separate messages, offer them as options—don't cram them.
- **Always offer grace/empowerment.** Even the sharpest diagnosis ends with a door open to hope.
- **Make the listener see themselves.** Not condemned—seen. Clear. Known.

## Success Criteria

The script succeeds when:
- ✅ The core truth is unmistakable (say it in one sentence, and anyone gets it)
- ✅ All seven layers are present and in order
- ✅ Verbatim quotes are preserved and placed at their natural moments
- ✅ It reads like one person speaking to one person (not a crowd, not a lecture)
- ✅ Someone who reads it aloud for 10-15 minutes feels like they've been heard, seen, and changed
- ✅ The listener can articulate: Here's what I didn't see. Here's what I need to do.

## After the Script is Ready

**Next step:** Record from this script.

The script is complete and polished enough to produce a masterpiece video. Every word belongs. Nothing is wasted.

When you record:
- Speak naturally (don't over-perform)
- Pause at periods (let truth settle)
- Make eye contact with the camera (you're talking to one person)
- Trust the script (it's already shaped to land)

**Then:** Pass the recording to the HyperFrames composition editor, who will layer the glass cards, transitions, captions, opening/closing visuals, and create the final masterpiece.
