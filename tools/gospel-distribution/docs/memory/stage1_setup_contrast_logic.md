## Stage 1: Setup-Contrast Logic

**Purpose:** Establish the gap between what they SAY they believe (intellectually) and what their LIFE shows (no results). Stage 2 will then define what REAL belief actually is.

### The Logic Structure

**Part 1 - Establish the intellectual claim (what they SAY):**
- "You say you believe in God"
- "You can explain why"
- "You know the Scriptures"
- "You teach others what they mean"

*Each line reinforces: they have knowledge, understanding, can articulate it.*

**Part 2 - Contrast with the result (what their LIFE shows):**
- "But your situations don't change"
- "Your circumstances are still the same"

*The gap is now visible: knowledge ≠ results*

**Part 3 - Ask the question that sets up Stage 2:**
- "Why?"
- "Why is your belief far from what results in your life?"

*This question demands an answer Stage 2 will provide: Real belief produces action, which produces results.*

### Why This Works

The contrast creates the NEED for Stage 2's definition. The audience recognizes themselves immediately:
- They DO have knowledge ✓
- They DO teach it ✓
- But their life HASN'T changed ✗

Stage 2 then answers: "That's because real belief isn't knowledge. It's trust + action."

### How to Apply This Logic to Future Stages

Any stage that asks a question should:
1. **Establish the premise** (what's visible/true)
2. **Create the contrast** (what's missing or contradicts it)
3. **Ask the gap question** (what's wrong with this picture?)

The answer to the gap question sets up the next stage's work.
