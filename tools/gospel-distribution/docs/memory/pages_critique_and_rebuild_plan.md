# Pages Critique & Rebuild Plan

## The Core Issue

Your content deepening went from **professional** (B+) → **marketing-driven** (good technique, wrong direction).

The issue: We made these pages feel like a **sales pitch** when they should feel like a **ministry invitation**.

**Key difference:**
- Sales pitch: "Here's our amazing transformation. Join us to be part of it." (emotional, individualistic)
- Ministry invitation: "This is God's call. These are the three ways people answer it. Which one is yours?" (clear, inclusive, organizational + individual)

---

## What You Provided (Partnership Example)

Your edited partnership version is **excellent** because it:
- ✅ Stays clear and concise (no flowery language)
- ✅ Explains WHY each partner type exists (not just what they do)
- ✅ Maintains organizational partner option ("Organization (optional)" field)
- ✅ Asks a simple, powerful question: "Which kind of partner are you?"
- ✅ Focuses on the calling, not the benefit
- ✅ Uses "WHY" sections to educate, not persuade

It's **ministry framing**, not marketing framing.
