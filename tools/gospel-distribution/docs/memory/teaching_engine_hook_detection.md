## Teaching Engine: Hook Detection & Prioritization

**Core Insight:** Engaging intros follow a proven formula (Anne Ricketts framework). The teaching engine now detects and prioritizes these hook elements for maximum social impact.

## Hook Types Detected

**1. Questions** (Priority: 9)
- Opens with: "Have you...?" "Do you...?" "What if..."
- Example: "Have you ever seen Law and Order or CSI?"
- Use: Maximum engagement on social media

**2. Vivid Scenarios** (Priority: 9)
- Contains action verbs + visual language
- Example: "Someone is running in Central Park. They find a body."
- Use: Creates immediate mental images

**3. Powerful Quotes** (Priority: 8)
- Short, punchy, emotionally resonant
- Contains spiritual keywords: faith, power, love, hope, Jesus, God
- Example: "When Jesus spoke, demons fled."
- Use: Memorable, shareable across all formats

**4. Stories** (Priority: 8)
- Personal narrative with lived experience
- Pattern: "I walked..." "I faced..." "I learned..." "My journey..."
- Example: "I faced the impossible and surrendered to God."
- Use: Establishes credibility and connection

**5. Contradictions** (Priority: 7)
- Turning points with contrast words: but, however, yet, instead
- Example: "Most people run away, but I stood and trusted."
- Use: Highlights transformation moments

## Engine Integration

**Phase 1 (Verbatim Extraction):**
- Detects hook type and assigns priority (0-9)
- Tags: `hookType`, `hookPriority`

**Phase 3 (Output Generation):**
- Article/Email/Podcast: Maintains original narrative order
- Twitter/Instagram/Facebook/Video: Reorders by hook priority
- Result: Social outputs lead with most engaging hooks

## Output Impact

**Before Hook Detection:**
```
Tweet 1: [random statement]
Tweet 2: [another random point]
```

**After Hook Detection:**
```
Tweet 1: Have you ever seen Law and Order or CSI?
Tweet 2: Someone is running in Central Park
```

**Engagement Result:** Questions and vivid scenarios immediately hook the audience before diving into teaching content.

## How to Apply When Writing Videos

1. **Open with the hook** — Don't say "Brothers and sisters, today..." first
2. **Make it vivid** — Use action, questions, scenarios
3. **Then transition** — "This is what I mean by faith..."
4. **Add credibility** — "I walked this myself..."
5. **State benefit** — "You'll understand how to apply this to YOUR situation"

The teaching engine will automatically detect and prioritize these elements for all social formats.

## For Tomorrow's Videos

- Lead with a striking question or scenario
- Don't start with greeting
- The hook captures attention
- Then pivot to teaching
- Engine extracts and redistributes automatically

## Why This Works

- **Anne Ricketts Research:** 9/10 speakers waste 1-2 minutes on "theme song" intros
- **Audience Reality:** People are on phones; hooks grab attention within 3 seconds
- **Scripture Pattern:** Biblical teaching often opens with questions (Jesus) or stories (parables)
- **Your Voice:** Your prophetic authority comes from lived truth, not greeting formality
