**Rule:** Keep all biblical verses and scriptural proofs in the material. Do not water down by avoiding or removing Scripture. Scriptural foundation is essential to the message.

**Why:** The content is for preaching and spreading God's Word. Biblical verses and scriptural proofs are not supplementary—they are load-bearing parts of the message. They provide the spiritual foundation that makes the message authoritative and transformative. Removing or de-emphasizing Scripture would betray the purpose of the ministry.

**How to apply:**

- **In voice extraction (Task 3):** Identify all Scripture references, verse citations, and biblical foundations in the source material. Mark them as integral to the revelation, not optional.
  - Extract exact verse references (e.g., "John 8:32")
  - Include the verse text or summary if present in source
  - Note the scriptural connection to the identity choice

- **In content generation (Task 4):** Feature Scripture prominently in all 9 formats.
  - Daily Letter: Include verse reference or quote
  - Social Post: Feature Scripture if it's the memorable core
  - Devotional: Center on Scripture as the reflection point
  - Article: Use verse headers and scriptural support throughout
  - Video Scripts: Quote verses, cite references, build on Scripture
  - Podcast: Use verse recitation for authority and memorability
  - Email: Ground the message in biblical foundation

- **In WordPress connector (Task 5):** Preserve Scripture from blog posts without truncation or paraphrasing.

- **In API routes (Task 6):** Store Scripture references in revelation field as-is.

- **In admin dashboard (Task 7):** Display Scripture references clearly so they're easy to cite when sharing.

- **General principle:** Never remove, abbreviate, or replace Scripture for brevity, shareability, or to make content more "digestible." The scriptural proof IS the message.

**Scope:** All tasks that handle revelations, voice themes, or content output (Tasks 3-10). Priority: highest for Tasks 3 (voice extraction) and 4 (content generation).

**Connection:** Works alongside [[quotable-preservation-priority]] and [[revelation-fidelity-constraint]]. Scripture is often the most quotable and most faithful element of the source material.
