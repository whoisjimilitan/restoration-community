## THE HIDE FRAMEWORK

HIDE is the intentional pacing structure that makes 40-45 second reels land. It's not a template to fit words into—it's the journey shape that ensures the reel creates movement in the listener.

**Hook:** Grab attention. Recognition or confrontation. (3-5 seconds)

**Intrigue:** Make them FEEL the problem. Let it land. (8-12 seconds)

**Delivery:** Show the answer. One core insight. Clear. (12-15 seconds)

**Exit:** Whatever is natural to this truth. Could be: action, question, realization, invitation, or confrontation. (5-10 seconds)

**Key principle:** HIDE structures the pacing so something changes in the listener. The exit is authentic to the specific truth, not forced into a predetermined form.

## VERSION A: EXTRACT FROM LOCKED /PREACH

**Process:**
1. Read the locked /preach piece completely
2. Identify the strongest 40-second moment that captures the core

   - Delivery: The core insight
   - Exit: Specific action they can take
4. Tighten to 40 seconds (approximately 80-100 words)
5. Preserve the Scripture reference from the /preach

**Result:** A reel that emerges naturally from the teaching but optimized for short-form.

## VERSION B: BUILD FROM SCRATCH (PURPOSE-DRIVEN + HIDE)

**PHASE 0: ESTABLISH PURPOSE**
- Who is listening? What's their actual struggle?
- Why must I say THIS to THEM right now?
- What core transformation does the locked /preach deliver? (VERSION B must deliver the same transformation, different angle)

**PHASE 1: STUDY VOICE FROM LOCKED /PREACH**
- Read the locked /preach carefully
- This IS the speaker's voice. Refined. Intentional. Earned through 5 phases.
- Extract the voice patterns:
  - What specific words does this person choose? (offense, not frustration; harboring, not holding)
  - How do they use repetition? (for emphasis, landing truth, not filler)
  - How do they build specificity? (in your heart, on the other side of that wall, a feeling you live with daily)
  - How do they shift responsibility? (you're holding on, it will remain, because you're holding onto)
  - How do they structure consequences? (concrete, visceral, grounded)
  - How do they land conclusions? (direct action, specific calls, no hedging)
- Document these patterns. You will write VERSION B using these exact patterns.
- **This ensures VERSION A and B share the same voice. Different angle. Same speaker.**

**PHASES 2-3: DRAFT FROM PURPOSE (Using extracted voice patterns + Authentic engagement)**
1. Start with the real person and real need
2. **Sit with the locked /preach message deeply** — Understand what it's actually saying at its core
3. **Ruminate on the message like a human would** — What natural analogies, metaphors, insights emerge from genuine engagement with THIS specific teaching?
4. **Let the hook emerge from authentic engagement, not from formula** — Not "use a confrontation hook" but "what's the natural human entry point to this specific truth?"
5. Write the natural conversation using the voice patterns extracted from /preach
6. Let it flow. Let paragraph breaks, sentence variation, and pacing emerge organically
7. Write in THIS PERSON'S actual speech patterns (not generic human voice, not constructed, not engineered)

**CRITICAL:** Each hook should be DIVERSE because each message naturally suggests different entry points. Generic formulas (confrontation, probing question, bold statement, etc.) are what everyone does. You do authentic engagement with the specific content.

**PHASE 4: IDENTIFY EMERGENT HIDE**
1. Read what you wrote
2. Notice: Where does Hook naturally appear? Intrigue? Delivery? Exit?
3. Map it. Don't impose it.
4. This validates the structure is already there

**PHASE 5: SURGICAL TRIM TO 40-45 SECONDS (80-90 words)**
1. Identify sentences that carry personal connection → KEEP
2. Identify sentences that repeat information → candidates for cutting
3. Preserve power moments and one-sentence paragraphs
4. Cut scaffolding, not bones
5. Preserve breathing room for natural pacing

**PHASE 6: SELF-CRITIQUE**
- Does it have teeth? (confronting, clear, specific)
- Does connection remain? (listener feels seen, recognized, met)
- Is the same substance/transformation present as VERSION A?
- Does it sound like THIS PERSON (from /preach voice patterns)?

**PHASE 7: HUMAN SPEECH AUDIT (MANDATORY)**
- Run line-by-line audit for construction marks
- Check: Does each phrase match the voice patterns extracted from /preach?
- Any engineered phrase gets replaced with human speech IN THIS PERSON'S VOICE
- No construction marks leave this skill

**PHASE 8: VERIFY SUBSTANCE MATCH**
- Does VERSION B deliver the same core transformation as VERSION A?
- Different delivery? YES. Same substance? MUST BE YES.
- Same voice? YES. (Because it's built from /preach voice patterns)

**Exit authenticity:**
The exit should be whatever is NATURAL to that specific truth—action ("Forgive. Now."), question ("Are you ready?"), realization ("That's what changes everything"), invitation ("What would it feel like?"), or confrontation ("You know what has to happen"). Do NOT force it to be action-oriented. Diversity in exits prevents formula obviousness and preserves authenticity. But the exit must be in THIS PERSON'S voice.

## VERSION C: THE POSTED LETTER (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

VERSION C is the final piece published to website, Substack, email, and archive. It uses all /preach and /teach quality standards AND applies the masterprompt framework for maximum clarity and impact.

**THE VERSION C FRAMEWORK:**

**TITLE: Challenge the Core Assumption (Not name the problem)**
- Should contradict what the listener believes about themselves or their situation
- Should create the need to read by posing an unsettled question
- Examples: "You're Not Weak" (contradicts "I feel weak"), "Strength Isn't What You Think" (contradicts assumption about effort)
- Should NOT describe the problem: avoid "The Source of Your Exhaustion," "Why You're Stuck," etc.
- The title hooks; the body naturally continues from that hook

**BODY: Unfold the Truth as Discovery (Not as list)**
- Use the locked /preach as the foundation
- Order it so the reader DISCOVERS the truth, not reads it listed
- Start with what they're experiencing or believing → Reveal what that actually means → Show the mechanism → Show the stakes → Offer the source/solution
- Should read like someone THINKING THROUGH a realization, not DELIVERING facts
- Preserve every core truth from the raw quote
- No scaffolding, no explanation of what you're doing, no bridges (except those found IN the raw quote)
- Use breathing room and paragraph breaks to let truths land before the next one lands

**OPENER: Set Up Personal Discovery (When Needed)**

**PURPOSE OF OPENER:** Create conditions for transformation. Work must change lives, not just inform. Opener must validate and respect the reader BEFORE delivering the teaching, so revelation lands with power to change thinking and action.

- NOT every quote needs an opener — only when raw arrangement doesn't already open itself
- When used, opener must:
  - Be something Jimi would say unprepared to someone he respects
  - VALIDATE their experience first (acknowledge their pain, situation, reality)
  - RESPECT their agency and humanity (honor their choices, feelings, rights)
  - THEN name the actual mechanism or opposition (not vague, not "it doesn't work")
  - Show WHY the truth matters (gives reasoner something to unpack)
  - Use natural speech, no construction marks, no em dashes
  - Invite understanding, not deliver a lesson
  - Be clear and specific (clarity is non-negotiable)

**CRITICAL STRUCTURE FOR OPENER:**
- ✓ Start with THE PERSON, not the action ("A believer who operates..." NOT "Operating in the natural...")
- ✓ Echo the body's opening naturally (first line of body determines how opener should start)
- ✓ Do NOT add new framing concepts not in raw quote (let the body reveal the solution, not the opener)
- ✓ Flow directly from problem to mechanism (no bridging language like "here's what makes it...")
- ✓ Opener SETS UP the body, doesn't TRY TO EXPLAIN the solution
- ✓ Create coherence with what comes next (opener connects to body, not stands alone)
- ✓ Opener should feel like the beginning of a thought the body continues, not a separate statement

**Example (Quote #1):** "Unforgiveness is the trap satan sets. You don't see it as a trap. You think you're protecting yourself. You were hurt and it's your right to forgive or not."
  - Validates experience (you were hurt)
  - Respects agency (your right)
  - Names mechanism (don't see it, think protecting)
  - Sets up revelation with psychological safety
  - Reasoner feels SEEN before truth lands

**Example (Quote #2):** "A believer trying to be strong in the natural is no match for satan. Human strength doesn't come from our natural abilities."
  - Names opposition (satan)
  - Respects the struggle (acknowledges they're trying)
  - Shows mechanism (outmatched, wrong source)
  - Packed with meaning for reasoner to unpack

- If raw quote already flows with discovery, skip the opener and let arrangement do the work
- **NON-NEGOTIABLE:** Opener must create safety and respect so the revelation can TRANSFORM, not just inform.

**CLOSE: Land the Realization for Daily Recalibration (Not one-time action)**
- Should deepen the realization so it sticks and creates habit
- NOT prescriptive homework or external action
- NOT long constructed sentences or poetic language
- Use SHORT, PUNCHY SENTENCES (not one long balanced line)
- Use PLAIN LANGUAGE (not "the One who is" — say "God")
- Use NATURAL SPEECH (what would you say to someone you respect?)
- The close should make them FEEL the transformation, not just KNOW it
- Examples: "Stop trying to be strong. Start drawing from God. Everything shifts from there." or "You're not weak. You're just drawing from the wrong place." or "The moment you decide to draw from faith instead of trying harder, you'll feel the difference."
- The close mirrors the power of the body — it doesn't pivot to homework

**CONSTRUCTION MARK AUDIT FOR CLOSE (CRITICAL):**

- ✓ Does it use poetic language ("the One," "flows," "takes it down")? If yes, replace with plain speech.
- ✓ Is it engineered for landing effect? If yes, simplify to natural speech.
- ✓ Would you say this out loud to someone? If no, rewrite it.
- ✓ Does it sound like SPEAKING A REALIZATION or CRAFTING A MEMORABLE LINE? Must be the first.
