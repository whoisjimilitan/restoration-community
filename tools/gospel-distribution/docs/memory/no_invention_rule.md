# NO INVENTION RULE (FOUNDATIONAL)

**You can refine. You cannot invent.**

---

## THE RULE

Every sentence in /preach must come from the raw quote or be a direct refinement of it.

You have creative freedom in:
- Making language more concrete
- Testing opening options
- Clarifying phrasing
- Making it sound more natural

You have ZERO freedom in:
- Inventing new sentences
- Adding frames not in the raw
- Inferring meanings beyond what's stated
- Creating interpretive additions
- Improving the "message" with invented context

---

## THE BOUNDARY

**ALLOWED (Refinement):**
- Raw: "A believer does not have strength as long as he operates in the natural."
- Refined: Same statement, clearer language, same meaning ✓

**NOT ALLOWED (Invention):**
- Raw: "A believer does not have strength as long as he operates in the natural."
- Invented: "You have no strength in the natural. None. The moment you operate from natural understanding, you've already lost." ✗
  - (Added emphasis, added frame, added invented consequence)

**NOT ALLOWED (Inference):**
- Raw: Statement about natural vs. faith operation
- Inferred: "Your perspective determines your power" ✗
  - (Not explicitly stated, inferred from context)

**NOT ALLOWED (Completely Invented):**
- Raw: Nothing about this
- Invented: "That's not exaggeration. That's mechanism." ✗
  - (Completely made up to sound sophisticated)

---

## TEST BEFORE INCLUDING

**Question 1:** Is this statement in the raw quote?
- If NO → don't add it
- If YES → proceed to Question 2

**Question 2:** Is this a direct refinement of a statement in the raw quote?
- If YES → okay to include
- If NO → remove it

**Question 3:** Am I inferring or interpreting beyond what's written?
- If YES → remove it
- If NO → proceed

---

## WHY THIS MATTERS

1. **Inference and invention change the true meaning** — They alter the intent and spirit of the original message
2. **When meaning changes, the work is not valid** — A /preach that invents is not a valid /preach. It's a false representation.
3. **Invention changes the speaker's voice** — Each invented sentence dilutes authenticity
4. **Invention introduces AI construction** — Artificial frames, explanatory bridges, interpretive additions all signal generation
5. **Invention violates Master Evidence Rule** — Claims of "from the raw quote" become false
6. **Invention ruins everything downstream** — If /preach invents, /teach can't extract truthfully, everything fails

**CRITICAL:** If inference or invention change the meaning, intent, or spirit of the message, the work is not valid. Period. Discard it and start over.

---

## EXAMPLE: QUOTE #2

**Raw Position Two:**
"A believer does not have strength as long as he operates in the natural. The more we get into the natural, the more satan rises up and becomes bigger. On the contrary, the more we get into faith, the more satan becomes smaller. Unless you decide to look at life from God's point of view, you would live a defeated life and you would go to the grave prematurely. While death is at work upon those who operate in the natural, life is at work upon those who walk by faith, because Jesus Himself is the very Bread of life."

**WRONG /preach (INVENTED):**
✗ Added: "You have no strength in the natural. None. The moment you operate from natural understanding, you've already lost." (Not in raw, invented frame)
✗ Added: "Your perspective determines your power." (Inferred, not stated)
✗ Added: "That's not exaggeration. That's mechanism." (Invented for effect)

**CORRECT /preach (REFINED ONLY):**
✓ Used only: All statements from raw quote
✓ Refined for: Clarity, naturalness, concrete language
✓ Kept unchanged: The voice, the meaning, the phrasing

---

## THE CRITICAL DISTINCTION

**REFINING:** Taking what's in the raw and making it clearer, more concrete, more natural-sounding. Same meaning, better expression of it.

**INVENTING:** Creating new sentences, frames, interpretations, or meanings that aren't in the raw.

You have unlimited freedom to refine. You have zero freedom to invent.

If you're tempted to create something new: STOP. Use what's in the raw. Refine it. That's all the freedom you get.
