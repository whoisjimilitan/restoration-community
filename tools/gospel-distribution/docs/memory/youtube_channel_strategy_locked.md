**Purpose:** Main prophetic voice, uncompromised calling

**Format:** Standalone story videos  
**Frequency:** 1x monthly  
**Purpose:** Proof of God's redemption, personal authority, connect divine call to personal journey

## Teaching Engine Integration (LOCKED)

**How It Works:**
1. Teaching Engine processes sermon → 7 publication formats
2. One of those formats becomes "Series 2: Living Free" video content
3. Opening frame (60s) establishes why this message serves the calling
4. Content Engine output (10-15 min) delivers without reframing
5. Closing frame (30s) ties back to "call young people out of fraud to salvation"

**Critical Rule:** No force-fitting. If a teaching doesn't naturally serve the mission, it still airs (authenticity > artificial coherence). Opening/closing frames handle the positioning, not rewriting the content.

**Example:**
- Teaching Engine output: "A Contented Life" (about contentment, not fraud)
- Opening Frame: "Fraud promises quick gain. But it brings destruction. True gain only comes from contentment in Christ."
- Content: Teaching Engine version (about contentment, full depth)
- Closing Frame: "When you're free from fraud's hunger, contentment becomes possible. That's the freedom I'm calling you toward."

## Recording & Production Notes (LOCKED)

**Solo Production Model:**
- Record solo in natural environment (minimal editing)
- Goal: 1 video per day acceptable (no heavy post-production)
- No green screen/studio setup needed
- Positioning and opening/closing frames matter more than production quality

**Quality Standard:**
- Audio clear (lav mic or smartphone mic in quiet space)
- Lighting natural or soft indoor
- Framing: mid-shot, Brother Jimi in frame, direct eye contact
- No heavy color grading—keep it real
