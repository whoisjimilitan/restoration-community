**Rule:** Preserve verbatim quotable and memorable lines from source material. The spirit AND the specific wording both matter because they've been refined through personal deduction to form convictions.

**Why:** Brother Jimi's messages represent convictions formed through deep reasoning and personal experience. The specific way something is said is intentional—chosen because it's memorable and aids learning. Paraphrasing dilutes impact. Verbatim quotes preserve the full force of the conviction.

**How to apply:**

- **In voice extraction (Task 3):** Identify and prioritize quotable/memorable sentences from the source text. These should be extracted as-is, not paraphrased.
  - Look for: statements that feel resonant, memorable turns of phrase, powerful contrasts
  - Mark them for quotation in all downstream formats
  - Examples from Brother Jimi's style: "You are choosing right now." "This is who you're becoming." "The answer determines your life."

- **In content generation (Task 4):** Feature direct quotes prominently in all 9 formats.
  - Daily Letter: Open with or embed a memorable quote
  - Social Post: Lead with the most quotable line
  - Micro Insight: This format IS a quote—use the most memorable phrasing
  - Article/Video/Email: Use quotes as section headers or key statements
  - Podcast Moment: Memorability is critical for audio—preserve exact phrasing

- **In selection:** When multiple ways to express something exist, default to Brother Jimi's exact wording unless it compromises voice extraction guardrails (rare). The default should be "preserve verbatim."

- **In admin dashboard:** Display source quotes prominently so they're easy to copy/use.

**Scope:** All tasks that handle revelations, voice themes, or content output (Tasks 3-10). Priority: highest for Tasks 3 (voice extraction) and 4 (content generation).

**Note:** This honors the reality that Brother Jimi has already done the hard work of deduction and articulation. Our job is amplification through format, not rewriting.
