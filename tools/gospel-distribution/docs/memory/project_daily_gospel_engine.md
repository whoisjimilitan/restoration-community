# DAILY GOSPEL DISTRIBUTION ENGINE
## Complete Locked Blueprint

---

## 1. THE NAME & POSITIONING

**Name:** Real Thoughts on Real Faith

**Frame:** "Daily reflections from Brother Jimi. What God is showing me. Maybe it speaks to what you're learning too."

**What it IS:** A personal letter. Intimate. Human. Real. Not a lecture, not a devotional, not a ministry platform. Brother Jimi sharing what he's wrestling with and what God revealed — and the reader feels seen because it names what they were already carrying.

**What it is NOT:** A business. A course platform. A diagnosis. No pricing pages, no "join my community," no visible monetization anywhere.

---

## 2. THE READER

Derived from 9 actual Boba quotes processed through /preach and /teach:

**The Christian who is wrestling** — not someone who identifies as defeated, but someone who:
- Feels fear, anxiety, or pressure they can't name
- Carries spiritual knowledge that hasn't become transformation
- Struggles to hear God's voice in the noise
- Feels their faith isn't producing what it should
- Is carrying something they haven't handed over yet

They come because they feel **seen**. They share it because it names something real for someone they love. That's the engine of growth.

---

## 3. CONTENT STRUCTURE (Every Daily Piece)

Each day produces **one complete letter** following this exact structure:

```
OPENING LINE
One sentence. Intimate. The hook. Sounds like a person talking.
"I was sitting with this thought this morning..."
Makes the reader lean in before they even decide to read.

THE WRESTLE
What caught me. What I was sitting with.
Removes the preacher-on-pedestal distance.
The reader thinks: "This person is real."

THE TRUTH
The core insight from /preach — refined through all 5 phases.
Defensible. Deep. Clear. Not invented.
This is what God showed me.

THE APPLICATION
What this means today. Specific. Liveable. Not abstract.
One thing they can do or hold onto before the day ends.

THE SEND-OFF
One line. A blessing. Lands and stays.
The emotional close. What they carry away.

---
THE QUOTABLE
One pull-quote extracted from the piece.
Standalone — makes complete sense without the letter.
Short enough to send in one screenshot or message.
So true that the reader immediately thinks of someone who needs it.
This is what they share. This is what travels.
```

---

## 4. THE /PREACH AND /TEACH OUTPUTS — WHAT THEY ARE AND WHERE THEY GO

### /preach produces:
The full refined letter — Phases 1 through 5 completed, all evidence logged. This is the **core teaching piece**. This is what becomes the letter on the website, the Substack post, and the email.

### /teach produces TWO versions:

**VERSION A — The Extracted Reel**
- Pulled directly from the /preach output
- 40 seconds, speaks like a human, flows like the letter
- The listener feels like Brother Jimi is talking to them personally
- Tone: reflective, warm, revelatory
- Use: embedded in the website letter page + in the Substack post
- Purpose: deepens the connection for people already reading. They read, then watch, then subscribe.

**VERSION B — The HIDE Reel**
- Built fresh from the raw Boba quote using the HIDE framework:
  - **H**ook — a promise or sharp opening that stops the scroll
  - **I**ntrigue — the feeling of the problem, the tension
  - **D**elivery — one core insight, clearly stated
  - **E**xit — one specific action or thought to carry away
- Tone: punchy, direct, designed for discovery
- Use: posted to Twitter/X, Instagram Reels, TikTok
- Purpose: reaches NEW people who don't know Brother Jimi yet. The HIDE structure is built to stop a scroll. VERSION B is the discovery engine.

### Layout on the Website Page:
```
[Opening Line — large, premium typography, full width]

[The Quotable — pulled out, styled differently, visually distinct]

```

### Layout on Substack:

## 6. THE SUBSTACK STRATEGY (Full Detail)

Substack is the invisible revenue engine. Here is how it works:

### Free Tier (Live from Day 1)
- Every day's letter + VERSION A reel published as a free Substack post
- Every website visitor sees a simple email signup: "Get tomorrow's reflection →"
- That signup feeds the Substack list
- Substack sends the email automatically every day
- The reader gets the letter in their inbox — they don't have to remember to visit the site
- **This is the relationship.** Inbox = trust = loyalty = future support

### Paid Tier (Created Day 1, Hidden Until Day 100)
- On Day 1, create the paid tier at $12/month
- Set it to CLOSED — nobody sees it, nobody is asked about it
- For 100 days: build trust, deliver value, ask nothing
- On Day 100: open the paid tier
- Send ONE email (the only time money is ever mentioned):

> "For 100 days, I've sent you what God has been showing me. I haven't asked for anything. I'm not starting now. But if this work has meant something to you — if one letter landed at the right moment — you can support it here. Everything stays free. Always. This is just for those who want to."

- Flip the switch. Never touch it again.
- Substack handles everything: billing, payments, receipts, renewals
- Your job: keep writing. Keep approving. The rest is automatic.

### Why This Works
- No one feels sold to because they weren't
- The 100-day relationship earns the right to receive support
- It's framed as optional generosity, not a transaction
- Substack is trusted — people already pay for Substack newsletters
- The content is the same free or paid — no gatekeeping — so supporting feels like patronage, not purchase

## 9. THE AUTOMATION PIPELINE

### Daily Trigger
Gmail API checks for Boba's daily quotes (arrives ~6pm). Detects new message. Extracts the 3 quote sets automatically.

### Processing
- /preach runs on each set (all 5 phases, full evidence logged)
- /teach runs on each set (VERSION A extracted + VERSION B HIDE-built, full evidence)
- All phase evidence saved to file — nothing posted without it

### Approval Email
Sent to whoisjimi.today@gmail.com automatically.
Contains:
- Full refined letter
- VERSION A reel script
- VERSION B reel script
- All phase evidence (proof every step was run)

Subject: "Today's reflections ready — [DATE]"

### Your Response (5 minutes)
Reply "Approve" — everything publishes
Reply "Redo #2" — that piece gets revised, rest publish
That's your entire daily involvement.

### Auto-Publishing (Triggered by Your Reply)
Gmail API detects reply → triggers:
- Website: Notion page created → Nooxy serves it live at brotherjimi.com
- Substack: API post published to free list
- Social: Buffer queues VERSION B reel + Quotable for morning posting
- Email: Substack sends digest automatically to all subscribers
