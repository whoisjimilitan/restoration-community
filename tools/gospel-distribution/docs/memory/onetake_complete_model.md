# One Take — Complete Business Model & Positioning

**Date:** September 8, 2026  
**Status:** Final agreed architecture (overrides all prior One Take docs)

---

## The Business Concept

**What One Take Is:**
An exclusive, invite-only cinematic property film studio that converts existing listing photos into cinematic video experiences that make luxury property buyers FEEL the space before they visit.

**Core Promise:**
"Your buyer is out there. They just haven't FELT your listing yet."

**The Mechanism:**
1. Hunt expensive luxury listings (Sotheby's, Zillow, MLS)
2. Download their photos + property details
3. Generate cinematic videos from photos using Higgsfield AI
4. Stitch videos into 6-chapter scroll-driven journey (backyard → rear → kitchen → bathroom → living room → bedroom → front → 180° reveal → aerial)
5. Deploy on 7-day temporary URL
6. Email agent personalized invite
7. Agent experiences free cinematic film
8. Agent chooses: keep it (£59/mo), buy it (£250), or add next property

## The Acquisition Funnel (Least Salesy)

### Stage 1: The Email (Curiosity Hook)
**Goal:** Get the click (zero friction)

**Subject:** "Your [Property] — one thing stood out"

**Body:**
```
Hi [Agent],

We analyzed your [Property Address] listing.

Most luxury listings rely on static photography. Yours has great bones, 
but buyers aren't FEELING the space before they visit.

We built something to show you what that feels like: [7-day temp link]

See what we mean.

— One Take
```

**Why:** Personalized observation, not salesy, gives value with zero ask.

### Stage 2: The 7-Day Temporary Page (The Proof)
**URL:** `onetake.com/[property-slug]-[date]`  
**Expires:** 7 days later

**What They See:**
- Hero section: Property name, location, price (minimal copy)
- Full 6-chapter cinematic scroll journey
  - Chapter 1: Backyard drone opening
  - Chapter 2: Enter through rear of house
  - Chapter 3: Kitchen cinematic glide
  - Chapter 4: Bathroom intimate moment
  - Chapter 5: Living room architectural reveal
  - Chapter 6: Bedroom → front → 180° exterior reveal → final drone
- At the end: Three-option close (see below)

**Why:** Pure proof. No pitch. No homepage clutter. Just experience.

### Stage 3: The Three-Option Close (Genius Close)

**Option 1: Keep It (£59/month recurring)**
```
"Your [Property] film stays live on our platform.

We guarantee more qualified interactions than your standard portal.

You get hosting, analytics, lead capture, and MLS distribution — all handled.

Keep it."
```

**Option 2: Own It (£250 one-time)**
```
"Take the files. Host it yourself. You own the asset forever."
```

**Option 3: Next Property**
```
"We're an invite-only studio. We elevate a handful of premium properties each month.

Which of your other premium listings would you like elevated?

[Form: Name, Agency, Next property URL or description]"
```

**Why This Works:**
- Option 1 = recurring revenue (£59/mo per property)
- Option 2 = one-time revenue (£250)
- Option 3 = upsell path (agent expands incrementally)
- Most agents choose Option 1 or 3 (both lead to revenue)
- Incremental upsell = one property/week staggered = sustainable growth

### Stage 5: The Phone Call (Conversation, Not Pitch)

**Agent fills homepage form or replies to email**  
**You call within 24 hours**

**Opening:**
> "Thanks for reaching out. I want to understand your world first. When do you typically list premium properties? What's your biggest challenge with buyer engagement on luxury listings?"

**Listen. Ask follow-ups:**
- "How many viewings does a property like that typically get in the first week?"
- "Do you ever lose deals because buyers didn't feel the space?"
- "Which of your properties are premium enough for this treatment?"

**Listen, don't pitch.**

**After listening:**
> "Here's what I'm thinking. You loved what we built for Addison Road. We can elevate your other premium listings the same way.

> £59/month keeps it live on our platform with guaranteed more interactions. Or £250 to own the files outright.

> Most agents start with one more and expand as they see results. Which property would you want us to elevate next?"

**Why:** They're already sold (saw the film). You're just confirming. Natural conversation, not hard sell.

## The Revenue Model

**Per Agent LTV Potential:**

- Average luxury agent: 5-8 premium properties at any time
- 1 property per week build capacity = 4/month
- First property: £59/mo (keep) OR £250 (own)
- Properties 2-5: Incremental £59/mo adds

**Example:**
- Month 1: Addison Road (£59/mo starts)
- Month 2: Add Notting Hill (£118/mo)
- Month 3: Add Mayfair penthouse (£177/mo)
- Month 4: Add 2 more (£295/mo)

**LTV per agent:** £295/mo indefinite (assuming 5 properties)

**Annual Revenue per Agent:** £3,540/year (one agent with 5 films)

---

## The Scarcity Model (Real, Not Fake)

"We're an invite-only studio. We build ONE cinematic film per week.

Your Addison Road film is done. If you want Notting Hill next, we'll slot you in this week. If not, it's next month.

We don't do volume. We do premium."

**Why this works:**
- Creates urgency for each property individually
- Agents can't bulk-buy (forces staggered upsell)
- Maintains "exclusive" positioning
- Capacity constraint = real scarcity

## Technical Architecture

**Key Components:**

1. **Email Template Engine**
   - Personalized curiosity hooks
   - Property-specific observations
   - Direct link to 7-day temp page

2. **7-Day Temporary Page Template**
   - Masthead (property name, location, price)
   - 6-chapter scroll journey
   - Video scrubbing (poster + video fallback)
   - Lenis smooth scroll + GSAP ScrollTrigger
   - Three-option close at end
   - Expires after 7 days

3. **Homepage (Permanent)**
   - Five scroll sections (framings)
   - No specific property showcases
   - Pure positioning
   - Form to collect leads

4. **Higgsfield Integration**
   - Converts property photos → cinematic videos
   - One video per sequence (8-10 videos per property)
   - Consistent visual language across property

## Positioning Summary

**The Core Insight:**
Luxury agents don't buy because of technology. They buy because their properties will convert faster and get more viewings.

**The Framing:**
"Your listing isn't selling because buyers haven't FELT it. We make them feel it before they visit. More feeling = more viewings = more sales."

**The Close:**
Not "impressive technology." Just: "Your Addison Road film got 3x more interactions than your portal. Imagine that for your other properties."

## What's Different from Balent's Model

Balent: "Build website (5 min) → 7-day temp deploy → agent keeps it (£59/mo) or buys (£250) → upsell to £500/mo for reviews+rankings+website"

One Take: "Build film (2-3 hrs via Higgsfield) → 7-day temp deploy → agent keeps it (£59/mo) or buys (£250) → upsell next property (£59/mo) → eventually £300+/mo from one agent"

**Key Differences:**
- Higgsfield automates video generation (our moat)
- Multiple properties per agent (higher LTV)
- Visual proof upfront (film beats website template)
- Recurring per-property model (sticky)
