**Keeps:**
- Narrative arc and flow
- Prophetic voice
- Story-based reasoning
- Theological depth

**Removes:**
- Pressure techniques ("Are you ready?")
- Sales qualification language
- Urgency framing
- Manipulation tactics

**Result:** Pages that feel like ministry invitations with narrative depth, not stripped-down simplicity.
