**Rule:** Revelations extracted from source material must remain completely intact. What we extract and generate cannot contradict or reinterpret the original material's meaning.

**Why:** The identity-centered content engine exists to amplify Brother Jimi's actual revelations from his source material (WordPress, podcasts, observations). The system is a formatter and multiplier, not an interpreter. Adding or changing meaning betrays the authenticity of the ministry and contradicts the core principle: "never alter Brother Jimi's voice."

**How to apply:**
- In voice extraction (Task 3): Extract revelations verbatim or as close as possible. Don't paraphrase in ways that shift meaning.
- In content generation (Task 4): All 9 formats must preserve the exact revelation. Use formatting and context to highlight, not to reinterpret.
- In validation: If a generated format contradicts, reinterprets, or changes the meaning of the source material's revelation, it fails.
- Add explicit checks: When extracting revelation sentences, verify they are quotes or direct paraphrases that preserve the exact meaning.

**Scope:** Applies to all tasks that work with revelations - especially Tasks 3 (voice extraction), 4 (content generation), 5 (WordPress connector), and the admin dashboard display.
