Do not write conditional, hedging, or persuasion-technique copy — this includes "it's not X, it's Y" rhetorical structures, soft conditional appeals like "if this is your story too," "if you identify with this," AND generic therapy/self-help reassurance phrases like "you don't have to carry this alone," "you're not alone in this," "it's okay to struggle."

**Why:** The user's established voice is short, blunt, declarative, raw, and prophetic — it states truth directly rather than inviting gentle self-identification or offering emotional comfort language. Real examples already praised as authentically his voice: "This would have been my end. A fool's end." / "Fraud is not just a crime. It is a spirit." / "Take this as a warning." None of these hedge with "if you feel this way" framing, and none reach for therapeutic reassurance — they declare. A closing CTA that says "If this is your story too, Jesus is the way out" softens into motivational-speech register and was flagged directly: "clarity, coherent, bold, raw, prophetic... not pacifying even though my page still shows empathy without motivational speeches." Separately, when a `/get-help` rewrite was proposed with the line "You don't have to carry this alone," it was flagged even more sharply: "I told you this is a ministry not a self help medium. This is not a motivational medium. I don't even speak like that." That phrase has no conditional structure — it's a plain declarative sentence — and was still wrong, because the register itself (therapist-comfort language) is foreign to his voice, independent of sentence structure. The fix in both cases: drop the softening language entirely and state the declaration — "Jesus is the way out." full stop, no lead-in, no comfort-phrase substitute.

**How to apply:** Before finalizing any CTA, headline, or closing line, check for: (1) "it's not X, it's Y" persuasion structure, (2) conditional/hedging openers ("if this is your story," "if you identify," "if you're struggling"), (3) therapy/self-help reassurance vocabulary regardless of sentence structure ("you don't have to carry this alone," "you're not alone," "it's okay," "healing takes time") — this register is wrong even as a plain declarative sentence, because the site is a ministry medium, not a self-help or motivational medium, (4) generic self-help vocabulary that could apply to any testimony, not specifically his. When in doubt, cut the preamble/reassurance and lead with the direct declarative truth-statement instead — usually something naming Jesus's action, not the visitor's emotional state. This applies even when the conditional framing already exists elsewhere on the site (e.g. /get-help's pre-existing headline) — pre-existing instances aren't automatically exempt, they're just lower priority to revisit unless he flags them.
