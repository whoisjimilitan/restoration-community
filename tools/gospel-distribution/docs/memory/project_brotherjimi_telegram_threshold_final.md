# Brother Jimi Telegram Threshold — Final Design

**URL:** /bro-jimi

**Status:** Ready to deploy. Waiting on real Telegram invite link.

## What Makes This Premium

**Claude Design's structural insight + Our typographic elevation = Genuinely premium.**

### Claude Design Contributions
- **Viewport centering** — Composition sits vertically centered instead of top-pinned, creating one complete moment rather than content-that-trails-off
- **Solid gold CTA** — Changed from outline to filled gold with dark green text. The page has one job; the button doing it must be unmissable, not whisper-quiet
- **Play button contrast fix** — Dark icon on gold (instead of pale-on-pale)
- **Animation stagger** — ~100ms between mark → headline → audio → button. Reads as "considered" without announcing itself
- **Portrait desaturation** — Restored filter so thumbnail stays cohesive with muted palette
- **Accessibility** — Play button label switches to "Pause," progress bar has screen-reader semantics

### Our Typographic/Spacing Elevation
- **Negative letter-spacing** on headline (-0.025em) for authority
- **Hairline borders** replacing all shadows (1px rgba(255,255,255,0.12))
- **Generous spacing** — py-48 md:py-56, space-y-20 md:space-y-24
- **Alpha hierarchy** — Mark line 80% opacity, controls breathing (gap-0.5)
- **Frosted glass audio card** — Semi-transparent, soft borders, no drop-shadow weight

## Final Composition
1. Mark line: "BROTHER JIMI | A PRODUCT OF JESUS' GRACE" (subtle white, 80% opacity)
2. Headline: "Every day, the Word. / Every Friday, his story." (Fraunces serif, tight tracking, leading-relaxed)
3. Audio card: Profile photo + label + play/progress/time (integrated, frosted treatment)
4. CTA: "Enter on Telegram" (solid gold, dark green text, clear primary action)

**All animations:** Fade + lift on load, staggered, respects reduced-motion.

## What Was Learned
- **Vertical centering** matters more than perfect spacing alone
- **Solid CTA** > subtle outline when the page has one job
- **Premium is earned through intentionality**, not decoration
- **Accessibility and craft** are the same thing
- **Restraint compounded** (from typography, spacing, color, and animation) creates luxury feel
