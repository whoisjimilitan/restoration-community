## The Five-Stage Journey

The old (commit eab0d06) homepage had a powerful narrative arc that made visitors EXPERIENCE the journey instead of reading an EXPLANATION. This is the architecture to preserve while updating theology.

### 1. **Hero: Acknowledgement → Revelation → Solution → Belonging**
- Starts where visitor IS: "You already know what deception costs"
- Reveals deeper truth: "our deepest need is reconciliation with God"
- Names solution: "Through Jesus Christ, restoration is possible"
- Removes isolation: "no one should have to walk that journey alone"

**Why this works:** It's a 4-step emotional arc that takes the visitor deeper. Not explanatory—it shows understanding.

### 2. **Founder: Proof the Journey is Real**
- "I was once trapped like you" (connection)
- "fraud had its hooks in deep... weight of it was crushing me" (validation)
- "Jesus Christ delivered me completely" (transformation)
- "Now I help others... That's why this exists" (purpose)

**Why this works:** Shows the journey is possible and lived. Makes founder credible as guide.

### 3. **Why We Exist: Theological Clarity**
- Direct statement: "We exist because Jesus Christ alone can deliver people trapped in fraud and deception"
- Spiritual diagnosis: "Fraud is spiritual bondage"
- The journey: "After deliverance, restoration—rebuilding identity, relationships, life on truth"

**Why this works:** Clear but not preachy. Establishes the two-part reality: deliverance first, then restoration.

### 4. **Journey: Simplified Path Forward (3 Stages)**
- **Truth**: Acknowledge where you are, no hiding
- **Confession through Repentance**: Confess, turn away, receive forgiveness, rebuild identity
- **Reconciliation and Service**: Rebuild relationships, honest work, become evidence, help others

**Why this works:** Gives visitor a map they can follow. Shows what comes after the CTA.

### 5. **Final Invitation: Call to Confession**
- **Headline**: "Ready to Take the First Step?"
- **Copy**: "The first step is a confession. Open your heart to the possibility that Jesus Christ can deliver you. Take a moment to acknowledge where you are. God is listening."
- **Button**: "I Need Deliverance"
- **Reassurance**: "No registration. No pressure. Just the beginning of your encounter with Jesus Christ."

**Why this works:** Makes the first step feel safe and specific. It's not a sales form—it's opening your heart.

## What Changed in Current Version (Why It Lost Power)

Current version shifted to EXPLANATION instead of EXPERIENCE:
- Hero: "If fraud, scams, or dishonest living have become part of your life, there is a way out. Jesus Christ delivers, restores, and leads people into a new life of truth and purpose."
  - This *describes what the ministry does* instead of *inviting the visitor into the journey*
  - It explains benefits instead of creating recognition

**The Fix:** Preserve the 5-stage experiential arc (acknowledge → reveal → name → prove → invite) while updating the theology to clarify: fraud mission, Jesus clarity, deliverance vs restoration distinction, testimony as multiplication.
