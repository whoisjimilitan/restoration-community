## THE THREE FUNDING STREAMS

### **STREAM 1: MONTHLY PARTNERS (Operations)**
```
"Give monthly. Support the discipleship work. 
Let us handle the format, the timing, the details."

Partners give: $X/month (recurring, predictable)
What they fund: 
  - Brother Jimi's time + team
  - Prayer call infrastructure
  - Mentor training + support
  - Content production
  - Administrative overhead

Partners don't: Pick the event, specify the location, 
               decide who attends
               
Partners trust: That their money is doing 
               the discipleship work effectively
```

**Why this is better:**
- Predictable revenue (partners commit)
- Operational freedom (you choose format based on opportunity)
- No event-by-event justification needed
- Scales naturally (more monthly partners = more capacity)

**Tiers:**
- **Individual:** $50-200/month ("I support one person's journey per month")
- **Church:** $500-2,000/month ("We partner in discipleship")
- **Organization:** $1,000-5,000/month ("This aligns with our mission")
- **Foundation:** $5,000+/month ("We commit to this work long-term")

---

### **STREAM 2: PARTICIPANT SPONSORS (Direct Cost)**
```
"If you're coming, bring a sponsor. 
This isn't fraud money, it's clean money. 
Your family, your church, a mentor—someone 
who believes in your transformation enough 
to invest in these 7 days."

Participant brings: Sponsor
Sponsor pays: $500-$2,000 (location-dependent)
What it covers: 
  - Participant's seat in the program
  - Materials, meals, accommodation (if away retreat)
  
Participant pays: $0 (directly)
Reason: We don't take money from fraud
        We take it from those who believe in them
```

**Why this works:**
- Participant has zero financial barrier
- Sponsor is invested (they paid, they care about outcome)
- Built-in accountability (sponsor is watching transformation)
- No tainted money in the system
- Sponsor becomes informal mentor/accountability partner

**Example:**
```
Ahmed is in fraud trap. Calls Brother Jimi.
After prayer call, invited to 7-day intensive.

Ahmed cannot pay (fraud money, obviously).
Ahmed's sister believes in his transformation.
Sister sponsors him: $1,200 (covers food, lodging, materials).
Sister attends as observer or joins activities.
Sister becomes part of Ahmed's accountability system.

After 7 days: Sister sees transformation firsthand.
She becomes co-investor in his mentorship journey.
She might even become his formal mentor.
```

## FACILITATOR MODEL (Careful Discernment)

**NOT:** "Complete program = automatic facilitator"

**YES:** "Show spiritual fruit + Brother Jimi approves = opportunity to co-lead"

### **Progression:**

```
COMPLETED 7-DAY INTENSIVE
├─ Gets: Completion certificate (recognition)
├─ Does NOT get: Automatic facilitator status
└─ Status: "Growing in faith"

[MENTOR WATCHES FOR 2-3 MONTHS]
├─ Honesty in daily life?
├─ Trustworthy with money?
├─ Helping others without being asked?
├─ Changing actual behaviors (not just talking)?
└─ Relationship with God deepening?

IF YES on most: Brother Jimi offers co-facilitation
├─ "We see spiritual fruit in you"
├─ "Want to help us guide the next group?"
├─ "You'll work alongside me, not lead solo"
└─ "Your journey becomes their proof"

CO-FACILITATION ROLE
├─ Helps welcome new participants
├─ Shares personal testimony (how they changed)
├─ Assists Brother Jimi during teaching
├─ Models the 7 stages in real life
├─ Becomes informal mentor to new participants
└─ "Little they do becomes your testimony"

AFTER SUCCESSFUL CO-FACILITATION
├─ Deeper spiritual training
├─ Possible mentor role (formal)
├─ Possible prayer call facilitation? 
│  (Only if Brother Jimi discerns readiness)
└─ Path to elder/spiritual leader
```

**Why this matters:**
- Not everyone should facilitate
- Spiritual maturity is discerned, not automatic
- Co-leadership protects the integrity
- Their actions (not their attendance) prove transformation
- It's discipleship, not program completion

## YOUR ACTUAL BUSINESS MODEL

This is sustainable because:

1. **Recurring revenue** (monthly partners) = predictable operations
2. **Multiple revenue streams** (prayer calls, sponsors, optional retreat sponsors)
3. **Flexible program delivery** (school, church, retreat = different settings, same content)
4. **Low overhead** (no fixed venue, no permanent staff)
5. **Scalable without hiring** (co-facilitators come from graduates, not external)
6. **Content asset** (every cohort generates testimonies + YouTube revenue)
7. **Institutional partnerships** (schools, churches, companies become recurring partners)

By Month 6:
- 12 active monthly partners: $5,850/month

Entirely sustainable. Entirely mission-aligned.
