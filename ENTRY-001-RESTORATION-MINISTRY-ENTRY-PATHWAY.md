## Purpose

Define the authoritative first encounter between a person seeking restoration and the Restoration Community ministry.

**ENTRY-001 establishes that:**

1. Prayer is **not an internal feature** of the platform
2. Prayer is **the spiritual doorway** through which people discover the ministry
3. The platform exists to facilitate a journey from **seeking → encountering → surrendering → restoring → serving**
4. Account creation, onboarding, and community are **consequences of prayer encounter**, not prerequisites

This document prevents the platform from drifting into what it is **not**:
- ❌ A Christian social networking site
- ❌ A discipleship course people "enroll in"
- ❌ A community people "join"
- ❌ A program with gatekeepers and prerequisites

And establishes what it **is**:
- ✅ A mercy encounter where broken people meet God through prayer
- ✅ A restoration pathway for those willing to surrender and change
- ✅ A place where transformed people become witnesses

```

**Authoritative understanding:**
```
A person encounters God through prayer
    ↓

    ↓

    ↓

    ↓

    ↓

**Why this matters:**
The first model treats the platform as the primary value.
The second model treats **restoration as God's work**, and the platform as a faithful servant of that work.

## Core Ministry Principle

### Restoration Community Does Not Begin With Membership

Restoration Community begins with **a cry for help**.

The first question a seeker asks is **not**:
- "How do I join this community?"
- "What is the restoration journey?"
- "Who is my mentor?"

The first question is:
- **"Can God help someone like me?"**
- **"Can I really change?"**
- **"Is there someone who will pray for me?"**

Therefore:

**The primary invitation of Restoration Community is prayer.**

Not: "Join us."
But: **"We will stand with you before God."**

## Non-Negotiable Principles

### 1. Prayer is the Ministry's First Act of Welcome; Platform Participation Follows

**Principle:**
A person does not need to understand the entire ministry, commitment level, or journey before reaching out. Prayer is how the ministry welcomes the broken.

**In practice:**
- The first action should be simple and immediate: **"I need prayer."**
- No prerequisite knowledge required
- No account required
- No onboarding burden
- No profile to complete
- Just a vulnerable person reaching toward God through a trusted servant

**Why:**
Someone carrying shame, guilt, desperation, or fear will not navigate a complex system. They will leave before they ask.

The threshold must be low.
The welcome must be immediate.
The first gesture must be one of mercy: **We will pray with you.**

**Authority:**
GOV-002 Part 4 (Confidentiality Principle): "A person's vulnerability belongs first between the person and God and trusted servants."

This principle means the first encounter is **personal**, not **transactional**.

### 2. The Platform Never Becomes the Hero

**Principle:**
The platform must never communicate that it is the source of transformation. Jesus Christ is the source. The platform is a tool in service of that truth.

**In practice:**
Every page, every feature, every message must answer one question: **Who is the hero of this experience?**

- If the answer is: "the platform," something has drifted
- If the answer is: "the community," something has drifted
- If the answer is: "the mentor," something has drifted
- If the answer is: "Brother Jimi," something has drifted

The answer should always be:
- **Jesus Christ is the source of restoration**
- **The ministry serves His work**
- **The platform supports the ministry**

**What this prevents:**
- ❌ "Download our app and change your life"
- ❌ "Join our community and transform"
- ❌ "Our program will restore you"
- ❌ "This methodology will save you"

**What this enables:**
- ✅ "Jesus restores. This ministry walks with you as you seek Him."
- ✅ "We pray. God works. You are transformed."
- ✅ "Come as you are. Christ accepts you. The journey begins with prayer."

**When this applies:**
Every feature proposal, every hero section, every onboarding flow, every notification, every page—test it against this principle:

**Is Jesus the hero, or has the platform become the hero?**

If you cannot honestly say Jesus is the hero, the feature belongs in a different ministry or requires redesign.

**Authority:**
Four-Book Foundation, Book Three: "The platform exists to serve the ministry, not to become the ministry."

Scripture: "Unless the Lord builds the house, those who build it labor in vain." (Psalm 127:1)

---

### 3. The Ministry is Relationship-First, Not Registration-First

**Principle:**
The platform must not force a vulnerable person through unnecessary barriers before they encounter **care**.

**In practice:**
- Prayer request → Brother Jimi responds
- Not: Create account → Complete profile → Onboard → Request prayer
- Account creation is **a later step** in the relationship
- Onboarding is **after** someone has committed to the journey
- Journey participation is **after** they've encountered encounter

**Why:**
Registration systems are designed to **collect data** and **manage users**.

But the first phase of ministry is **relationship building**, not **data collection**.

Someone asking for prayer needs to experience:
- ✅ "Someone heard me"
- ✅ "Someone cares"
- ✅ "Someone is praying for me"

Not:
- ❌ "Please complete your profile"
- ❌ "Choose your preferences"
- ❌ "Agree to terms"

The registration happens **after** they know they are loved.

**Authority:**
Book Three, Chapter One (Digital Restoration Philosophy): "The platform exists to serve the ministry, not to become the ministry."

### 4. Jesus Christ Is the Doorway; Brother Jimi Serves as the Ministry's Pastoral Contact

**Principle:**
Jesus Christ is the doorway to restoration. The platform does not replace the servant of God. The platform **facilitates connection** to a faithful shepherd who points seekers toward Christ.

**In practice:**
- Prayer requests are received by **Brother Jimi's prayer ministry**, a servant of Christ
- Not to an automated system
- Not to a community prayer wall
- Not to an algorithm
- To **a person who prays and points to Jesus**

- Initial response is **personal and pastoral**
- Not automated confirmation email
- Not chatbot
- Not form letter
- A **human response** that guides toward Christ

- Spiritual authority remains **with Christ and the ministry serving Him**
- Teaching points to Christ
- Mentoring points to Christ
- Pastoral care shows Christ's love
- Discipleship leads to Christ

The platform is a **tool** that connects people to the ministry.
The ministry is the **servant** that connects people to Christ.
Christ is the **source** of restoration.

**Authority:**
GOV-002 Part 2 (Prayer Ministry Leadership): "Brother Jimi serves as the spiritual covering and authority for prayer ministry" — always pointing seekers toward Jesus Christ as the source of restoration.

## Participant States

The platform must recognize and honor **distinct participant states**.

Each state has different needs, access, and expectations.

### State 1: Visitor

**Who:** A person exploring, learning, wondering.

**Access:**
- Learn about Restoration Community
- Request prayer (without account)
- Read testimonies
- Understand the restoration philosophy
- Explore the mission

**Experience:**
- Homepage explains the ministry
- Prayer entry is visible and accessible
- Testimonies show transformation is real
- No pressure to commit

**Does NOT include:**
- ❌ Journey access
- ❌ Community participation
- ❌ Mentoring relationship
- ❌ Accountability structures

**Duration:** Open-ended. A visitor remains a visitor until they request prayer.

### State 2: Prayer Seeker

**Who:** A person who has requested prayer. The ministry has begun relationship.

**Access:**
- Prayer is being offered by Brother Jimi's prayer ministry
- Initial response from ministry
- Possible virtual encounter with ministry servant
- Prayer status/confirmation

**Experience:**
- Ministry responds personally
- Prayer is happening
- There is conversation and relationship building
- Initial encounter begins (confession, surrender, readiness)

**Does NOT include:**
- ❌ Journey access
- ❌ Community participation
- ❌ Participant status

**Duration:** From prayer request through initial encounter (typically days to weeks). Prayer seeker transitions to participant when they commit to restoration journey.

### State 3: Restoration Participant

**Who:** A person who has entered the seven-stage restoration journey.

**Access:**
- Full journey access (current stage + progression)
- Reflection submission
- Prayer support during journey (PRAYER-001)
- Mentoring relationships
- Community support
- Resource access

**Experience:**
- Walking the stages with ministry support
- Growing in restoration
- Encountering God through each stage
- Building new identity in honesty

**Duration:** Seven stages (length varies per person). Continues until transformation is realized.

### State 4: Restored Servant

**Who:** A person who has walked restoration and now serves others.

**Access:**
- Full participant access (ongoing support)
- Mentoring others
- Testimony sharing
- Service opportunities
- Possible prayer warrior role

**Experience:**
- Helping others enter restoration
- Witnessing God's work in new participants
- Growing in maturity
- Being part of the multiplication loop

**Duration:** Ongoing. A restored servant continues growing and serving.

## The Homepage Authority

Future website experience must align with this entry model.

**Current problem (NOT AUTHORIZED):**
```
Homepage Hero: "Join Restoration Community"
Subheader: "Walk the seven stages of restoration with mentor support and community"
CTA: "Create Account"
Result: Seekers see bureaucracy before they see welcome
```

**Authorized messaging direction (principles only; implementation deferred):**

The homepage must answer the **real question** seekers are asking:
- "Can God help someone like me?"
- "Can I really change?"
- "Is there hope?"

Not the question the platform is asking:
- "Aren't we amazing?"
- "Look at our features"
- "Here's how to sign up"

The hero should communicate:
- **There is hope**
- **God restores**
- **People have changed**
- **You are not alone**
- **If you are ready, begin with prayer**

The primary CTA should be something like:
- **"I Need Prayer"** (not "Join" or "Learn More")

The community is the **destination**.
Prayer is the **doorway**.

## Acceptance Criteria

ENTRY-001 is complete when:

✅ **Prayer is established as the front door of the ministry**
- Not a feature inside the journey
- Not a community prayer wall
- The first invitation to seeking people

✅ **Restoration Journey is established as the growth pathway after encounter**
- Accessible only after prayer encounter and commitment
- Supported by community and mentoring
- The discipleship structure

✅ **Community is positioned as support, not the initial attraction**
- Participants experience community
- But community is consequence of prayer, not cause
- Community strengthens the journey; doesn't begin it

✅ **Brother Jimi's ministry role is preserved**
- Not replaced by platform automation
- Not diluted by social features
- Central to every participant's encounter

✅ **Account creation is deferred appropriately**
- Not the first step
- Happens after prayer encounter
- Participant creates account when they commit to journey

✅ **Participant states are clearly defined**
- Visitor, Prayer Seeker, Participant, Servant
- Each state has appropriate access and experience
- Technology honors these distinctions

✅ **Future website and product decisions must align with this pathway**
- Every design decision references ENTRY-001
- Every feature questions: "Does this serve seeking people or participants?"
- No feature is added that makes prayer harder to access

✅ **Technology cannot introduce a different entry model**
- Cannot drift into "join community first"
- Cannot add social features that compete with prayer entrance
- Cannot make registration a prerequisite for prayer

## What This Prevents

### ❌ AVOID: "Christian Social Platform" Drift

**Risk:** Platform gradually becomes Facebook-like:
- Community prayer wall (public)
- Prayer likes/comments (social)
- Leaderboards (metrics)
- Engagement metrics (gamification)
- Groups and forums (community-first)
- User-generated content (participant-driven)

**Prevention:** ENTRY-001 establishes that prayer is **private encounter with ministry**, not social broadcast.

### ❌ AVOID: Registration-First Model

**Risk:** Seeking people encounter barriers before welcome:
- "Create account" (registration form)
- "Complete profile" (personal questions)
- "Choose interests" (preference collection)
- "Agree to terms" (legal/bureaucracy)
- "Begin stage 1" (immediate launch into content)

**Prevention:** ENTRY-001 establishes that prayer precedes registration.

### ❌ AVOID: "Features First" Mentality

**Risk:** Building features that sound good but conflict with ministry:
- "Connect with others in prayer" (social)
- "Track your answered prayers" (metrics)
- "Prayer request categories" (complexity)
- "Share prayer victories" (testimony performance)

**Prevention:** ENTRY-001 establishes that every feature serves the **seeking → encountering → restoring → serving** pathway.
