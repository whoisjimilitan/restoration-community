# Code of Conduct

The Restoration Community is committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all contributors.

## Our Commitment

We are committed to creating a community that is respectful, inclusive, and safe for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected Behavior

- Treat all contributors with respect and courtesy
- Listen actively and with an open mind
- Provide constructive feedback
- Focus on what is best for the community
- Take responsibility for your words and actions

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated:

- Harassment, discrimination, or exclusion
- Disrespectful or derogatory comments
- Personal attacks
- Unwelcome sexual attention
- Disruption of discussions or work

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the community stewards.

## Enforcement

Community stewards will investigate reports and take appropriate action to maintain a respectful environment.

---

This community is built on mutual respect and shared purpose. We ask that all participants uphold these values.
