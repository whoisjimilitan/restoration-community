## The Three Layers Explained

### Layer 1: MISSION-001 (THE WHY)

**Question:** Why does this ministry exist?

**Answer:** 
> "The Restoration Community exists because we believe that no human system can ultimately free a person from the life of online fraud. Jesus Christ alone restores hearts, transforms lives, and gives people the power to walk in truth."

**Protects Against:**
- Building a generic "restoration program"
- Treating the platform as the saviour
- Making it about community, journey, or technology
- Losing sight that Christ is the source

**Never Changes:** This is the mission. It is permanent.

---

### Layer 2: GOVERNANCE (THE HOW)

**Question:** How do we protect the mission as we build?

**Examples:**
- GOV-002: "Prayer is the ministry's first act of welcome"
- ENTRY-001: "Prayer precedes platform participation"
- [Future] GOV-003: Mentoring principles
- [Future] GOV-004: Community principles

**Protects Against:**
- Features that make the platform the hero
- Prayer becoming social (metrics, likes, walls)
- Drifting from Christ-centered vision
- Technology replacing pastoral authority

**Can Evolve:** More governance documents as ministry grows, but rooted in MISSION-001.

---

### Layer 3: IMPLEMENTATION (THE WHAT)

**Question:** What technology do we build to serve the mission?

**Examples:**
- PRAYER-001: System that enables prayer request submission
- JOURNEY-001: Stages that embody restoration theology
- [Future] MENTORING-001: Platform for pastoral relationships

**Protects Against:**
- Building features that aren't authorized by governance
- Technical decisions made without ministry authority
- Solving problems that don't serve the mission

**Can Change:** Technology can be replaced, refactored, improved — as long as it serves the governance layer and the mission.
