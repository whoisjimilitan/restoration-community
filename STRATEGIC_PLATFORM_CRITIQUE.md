# Brother Jimi Platform — Strategic Critique & Recommendations
## Multiple Perspectives: Strategist + UX/Designer + 8 Personas

**Date:** July 31, 2026  
**Status:** CRITICAL REVIEW BEFORE BUILD

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**The Good:** The core 7-stage restoration narrative is theologically sound, emotionally compelling, and addresses a real market gap (fraud → deliverance → service). The landing page nails the problem-awareness phase.

**The Gap:** The platform conflates three separate things (individual restoration, institutional partnerships, scaling infrastructure) without clarity on WHO the program serves first and HOW you'll reach them at scale.

**The Risk:** If SCOAN falls through, you have no fallback. If you're location-dependent, you can't scale. If you only reach individuals, partnerships won't have proof of system.

**The Opportunity:** Reframe from "cohort at SCOAN" to "scalable deliverance network" that serves individuals AND institutions simultaneously.

## SECTION 2: THE STRUCTURAL GAPS 🔴

### **GAP 1: Location Dependency**

**Current Model:**
```
All participants → SCOAN Accra, Friday 3pm
```

**Problems:**
- SCOAN hasn't responded (critical blocker)
- Geographic barrier: Only Accra residents can attend
- Scale impossible: 15 people/week forever at one location
- No contingency if SCOAN declines

**Better Model:**
```
Hybrid Physical + Virtual + Decentralized

Week 1-3: Core gathering (can be anywhere)
Week 4-7: Mentored small groups (can be virtual)
Ongoing: Prayer for Healing (100% digital)

Scale path:
- Month 1: Cohort 1 at location A
- Month 2: Cohort 2 at location B (replicate model)
- Month 3: Cohort 3 at location C
- Month 6: 6 cohorts running in parallel
```

**Design implication:** Dashboard must support MULTIPLE locations/cohorts, not assume SCOAN.

---

### **GAP 2: Partnership Audience Confusion**

**Current Assumption:** "Individuals + some donors"

**Reality Check:** 
Who SHOULD fund this?
1. **Individual donors** — Moved by personal testimony (current model)
2. **Institutional partners** — NGOs, churches, government programs
3. **Corporate social responsibility** — Banks, telcos, tech companies
4. **Faith organizations** — Denominations, prayer movements
5. **Government programs** — Youth rehabilitation, fraud prevention

**Problem:** You're not messaging to institutions at all.

**Better Position:**
```
NOT: "Help us help individuals in fraud"
YES: "Partner with a proven deliverance system"
     "Integrate restoration into your organization"
     "Reduce fraud recidivism through spiritual transformation"
```

**Example target partners (Ghana):**
- GhIPCC (Ghana Internet Payment Fraud Prevention Council)
- National Youth Authority
- Churches (ICTS, Free Gospel, Pentecost, etc.)
- Tech hubs (running youth training → need exit-to-integrity)
- Banks (fraud recovery + offender rehabilitation)
- Prisons (rehabilitation program inside institutions)

**Design implication:** Need TWO partnership tracks on website:
1. Individual sponsor ("Help one person per month")
2. Institutional partner ("Integrate into your organization")

### **GAP 3: Target Audience Clarity**

**Current framing:** "Young people in fraud"

**Who are they really?**
- Age? (16-25? 18-35?)
- Gender? (Primarily male? Mixed?)
- Geography? (Urban only? Rural?)
- Language? (English speakers? Need Twi/Ga?)
- Income level? (Unemployed? Underemployed?)
- Faith background? (Christian-curious? Committed? Secular?)
- Entry point? (Self-referred? Family intervention? Court-mandated?)

**Why it matters for design:**
- UI language changes based on age
- Onboarding flow changes if court-referred vs self-referred
- Content needs differ if faith-skeptical vs faith-seeking

**Better: Create 3 Primary Personas**

Based on your platform needing to serve three distinct entry points:

**PERSONA A: "The Individual Seeker"**
- Age: 22-32
- Situation: "I was scamming, now I'm trapped, I want out"
- Entry: Hears about ministry via friend, church, or Google search
- Motivation: Genuine spiritual desperation
- Barrier: Doesn't trust easily (been lying for years)
- Needs: Personal response from Brother Jimi, proof that change is possible

**PERSONA B: "The Family Intervention"**
- Age: Parent/relative of person in fraud (any age)
- Situation: "My son/daughter is caught up in scamming, I'm desperate"
- Entry: Searches "how to help child in fraud" → finds your site
- Motivation: Parental love, spiritual hope
- Barrier: Skeptical of ministry, wants tangible outcomes
- Needs: Case studies, transparency, clear expectations

**PERSONA C: "The Institutional Partner"**
- Role: Director of NGO, church leader, govt official
- Situation: "We serve youth at-risk. We need holistic solutions."
- Entry: Referral from other ministry, funding search, network
- Motivation: Mission alignment, proven methodology
- Barrier: Wants scalable system, not one-man show
- Needs: System documentation, outcomes data, training capability

**Plus 5 Secondary Personas** (we can define if needed)

### **GAP 5: Scaling Architecture**

**Current model:**
- One Brother Jimi
- One location (SCOAN)
- One mentor per cohort
- One 7-week cycle

**This doesn't scale to:**
- 100 people/week
- 5 locations
- Cohort 3, 4, 5...
- Government contracts

**Missing layer: TRAINING STRUCTURE**

You need to build your platform assuming:
- **By Month 3:** You need 5 trained facilitators, not 1 Brother Jimi
- **By Month 6:** You need to train church planters to run cohorts
- **By Month 12:** You need a certification program

**Design implication:** Admin dashboard needs:
- Facilitator management (not just Brother Jimi)
- Training/onboarding workflow
- Standardized curriculum (so Stage 1 runs the same in Accra, Kumasi, Takoradi)
- Quality control dashboard (Brother Jimi sees all cohorts, can audit)

## SECTION 3: PARTNERSHIP STRATEGY (THE MONEY)

### **Your Statement: "We don't beg, we let people feel led"**

This is RIGHT, but needs structure:

**Tier 1: Founding Partners (Now — Before Aug 8)**
- Commitment: 12-month sponsorship + monthly updates
- Give: $5,000-$50,000
- Get: Name on site, quarterly reports, testimony access, impact metrics
- Who? Wealthy individuals + churches that know Brother Jimi personally
- **Action:** Personal outreach (not website) — this is relational

**Tier 2: Standing Partners (Ongoing)**
- Commitment: Recurring donation ($50-$500/month)
- Give: Any amount, automatic renewal
- Get: Monthly impact emails, access to prayer updates
- Who? Individual believers who want ongoing participation
- **Action:** Website signup + email sequence + giving portal

**Tier 3: Prayer Warriors (Free)**
- Commitment: Pray for the ministry + share testimonies
- Give: Prayer + social amplification
- Get: Prayer requests, testimonies, monthly prayer guide
- Who? Anyone who wants to support without money
- **Action:** Email list signup, WhatsApp prayer group

**Tier 4: Institutional Partners (Future)**
- Commitment: System adoption + funding + facilitation
- Give: $10,000-$100,000+ annually + staff time
- Get: Customized curriculum, facilitator training, outcomes reporting, co-branding
- Who? NGOs, churches, government programs
- **Action:** Direct partnership conversations (not website)

**Tier 5: Impact-Based Sponsors (Unique)**
- Commitment: "Sponsor one person's journey" ($X per cohort)
- Give: $200-$1,000 per person
- Get: Monthly updates on their person's progress, testimony when complete
- Who? Supporters who want tangible impact
- **Action:** Website feature + direct matching
