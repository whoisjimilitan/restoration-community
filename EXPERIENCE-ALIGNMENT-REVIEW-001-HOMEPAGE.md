# EXPERIENCE-ALIGNMENT-REVIEW-001
## Review 1: Homepage Messaging Alignment

**Question:** Does the homepage communicate deliverance through Jesus Christ, or does it accidentally sound like a community/programme?

**Test Standard:** The Four-Part Spine
1. Fraud is the mission field
2. Jesus Christ is the answer
3. Restoration is the journey
4. Testimony is the multiplication

**Guiding Question:** "If someone trapped in fraud arrives here, do they immediately understand that Jesus Christ is offering them a way out?"

---

## Current Homepage Audit

**Status:** This review requires inspection of the actual homepage.

**What needs to be reviewed:**

### 1. Hero Section
**Current state:** [Requires inspection]

**Should communicate:**
```
Restoration
Deliverance Through Jesus Christ for Those Trapped in Fraud

You do not have to remain trapped in the life you are living.
Jesus Christ can deliver and restore you.
```

**Test questions:**
- ✓ Is the primary heading simple and clear?
- ✓ Does it state the mission field (fraud)?
- ✓ Does it state the answer (Jesus Christ)?
- ✓ Does the supporting line address someone trapped?
- ✓ Does it offer hope without being patronizing?
- ✓ Is there visual hierarchy that guides the eye?

---

### 2. Primary CTA (Call to Action)
**Current state:** [Requires inspection]

**Should be:**
```
I Need Deliverance
```

**NOT:**
- ❌ "Join now"
- ❌ "Get started"
- ❌ "Create account"
- ❌ "Begin your journey"
- ❌ "I need prayer"

**Why this matters:**
When someone clicks "I Need Deliverance," they are:
- Recognizing their situation
- Asking for help
- Opening their heart to the possibility of change
- Beginning a confession, not joining a platform

**Test questions:**
- ✓ Is the CTA phrased as a recognition/response?
- ✓ Does it use the language the person arriving is thinking ("deliverance")?
- ✓ Is it visually prominent without being aggressive?
- ✓ Is it clear this is not a purchase or registration?

---

### 3. Supporting Content (Below Hero)
**Current state:** [Requires inspection]

**Should communicate (in order):**

**Section 1: Founder's Testimony (Visible, Not Hidden)**
```
Heading: A Message from Someone Who Walked This Road

"I was once trapped like you. Jesus Christ delivered me completely.
Now I help others receive their deliverance from Him."
```

**Why this matters:**
The person needs to know someone has walked this road and found freedom.
The testimony is not "look at my achievement" but "look at what Jesus did for me."

**Test questions:**
- ✓ Is the founder's story visible on the homepage?
- ✓ Does it communicate deliverance, not achievement?
- ✓ Does it point to Jesus Christ, not the founder?
- ✓ Is it positioned as part of the doorway, not biography?

**Section 2: What This Is (Simple Explanation)**
```
This is a ministry for people trapped in fraud and dishonest living.

Jesus Christ alone offers true deliverance and restoration.

We believe:
- Freedom from fraud requires a power greater than your own
- That power is Jesus Christ
- After deliverance, restoration is a lifelong journey
- Your transformed life becomes hope for others still trapped
```

**Test questions:**
- ✓ Does it name the mission field (fraud)?
- ✓ Does it position Jesus Christ as the unique answer?
- ✓ Does it avoid sounding like education/coaching/rehab?
- ✓ Does it mention restoration as a journey?

**Section 3: The Journey (Overview, Not Details)**
```
Heading: From Deliverance to Restoration

After encountering Jesus Christ and experiencing deliverance,
you walk a journey of restoration:

Truth | Confession | Repentance | Forgiveness | Reconciliation | Honest Work | Serving

Each stage helps you build a new life rooted in truth and purpose.
```

**Test questions:**
- ✓ Does it clearly state this is AFTER deliverance, not instead of it?
- ✓ Are the seven stages visible but not overwhelming?
- ✓ Does it avoid making the journey sound like a programme?
- ✓ Does it suggest serving as the natural outcome?

---

### 4. Visual Language and Tone
**Current state:** [Requires inspection]

**Should convey:**
- ✓ Hope (not despair or shame)
- ✓ Specificity (not generic spiritual language)
- ✓ Directness (not vague or abstract)
- ✓ Invitation (not judgment or interrogation)
- ✓ Jesus Christ at the centre (not the ministry, not the platform)

**Should NOT convey:**
- ❌ "Join a community"
- ❌ "Fix yourself with our help"
- ❌ "This is a sophisticated programme"
- ❌ "You need our platform"
- ❌ "Let us help you change"

---

### 5. Navigation and Secondary CTAs
**Current state:** [Requires inspection]

**Secondary elements that should NOT compete with primary CTA:**
- Partnership invitation should be small and secondary (not a main nav item)
- "About us" should be discoverable but not prominent
- "How it works" should redirect to the confession flow, not a feature list
- Social proof (if any) should show transformation, not numbers

**Test questions:**
- ✓ Does the primary CTA dominate the page?
- ✓ Are secondary CTAs clearly secondary?
- ✓ Is partnership visibly not the main ask?
- ✓ Does every navigation item serve the person seeking deliverance?
