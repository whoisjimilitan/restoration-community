   - Understanding: Moving toward peace with truth, humility, grace

   - Living: Integrity in employment, learning, service
   - Same complete structure

   - Biblical: Mark 10:45 (ESV)
   - Understanding: Purpose discovered through faithfully following Christ
   - Living: Service through gifts, humility, joy
