# EXPERIENCE-ALIGNMENT-REVIEW-001
## Framework for Translating Ministry Reality into Human Experience

**Purpose:** Ensure the public-facing experience of Restoration faithfully communicates the ministry reality already established.

**Not creating new identity. Not redesigning governance. Not inventing the journey.**

**Translating already-established truth into the human experience.**

---

## The Guiding Question

**"If someone trapped in fraud arrives here, do they immediately understand that Jesus Christ is offering them a way out?"**

Every decision in this review phase is tested against this single question.

---

## Core Principles (Already Established, Now Being Expressed)

### 1. Fraud is the Mission Field
This ministry has a specific assignment.

The person arriving recognises themselves in that assignment.

### 2. Jesus Christ is the Answer
Not education, not coaching, not community.

The person sees immediately that this points to deliverance through Christ.

### 3. Restoration is the Journey
After deliverance, not instead of it.

The person understands the pathway follows spiritual freedom.

### 4. Testimony is Multiplication
The founder's story says: "Jesus delivered me. Jesus can deliver you."

Not: "Look what I overcame."

---

## Review Sequence (In Order)

### Review 1: Homepage Messaging
**Question:** Does the homepage communicate deliverance, or does it accidentally sound like a community/programme?

**Scope:** Hero section, primary CTA, supporting text, visual hierarchy

---

### Review 2: CTA and Confession Flow
**Question:** After someone clicks, do they immediately begin confession, or do they hit registration?

**Scope:** First experience, form questions, tone, progression

---

### Review 3: Founder Testimony
**Question:** Is the founder's story central, and does it point to Jesus Christ?

**Scope:** Placement, content, emphasis, integration into doorway

---

### Review 4: Journey Presentation
**Question:** How and when are the seven stages introduced?

**Scope:** When stages appear, how they are framed, progression from encounter to restoration

---

### Review 5: Name Decision
**Question:** After expression is clarified, does the name still fit?

**Scope:** "Restoration Community" vs "Restoration" + descriptor

---

### Review 6: Partnership Placement
**Question:** Is partnership secondary to the primary ministry invitation?

**Scope:** Visibility, tone, timing, relationship to CTAs

---

## Language Standards (Going Forward)

### ❌ Avoid (Shifts the Centre)
- "Transform your life with our programme"
- "Join our restoration community"
- "We help scammers change"
- "Start your restoration journey"
- "Create an account to begin"

### ✅ Better (Centers Jesus Christ)
- "Jesus Christ can transform your life"
- "Begin your journey of restoration through Jesus Christ"
- "We point people trapped in fraud back to Jesus Christ, who alone delivers and restores"
- "Jesus Christ offers deliverance and restoration"
- "Open your heart to the possibility of freedom"

---

## Discipline for This Phase

**What we are NOT doing:**
- ❌ Creating a new ministry identity
- ❌ Creating new governance
- ❌ Redesigning the journey
- ❌ Inventing new theology

**What we ARE doing:**
- ✅ Making sure every experience element communicates the existing reality
- ✅ Ensuring the person's first encounter points to Jesus Christ
- ✅ Translating already-established truth into language and flow
- ✅ Removing accidental misalignment

---

## Success Criteria for This Phase

**When expression alignment is complete:**

✅ Someone arriving at the homepage immediately sees this is about deliverance through Jesus Christ

✅ The first interaction is confession/openness, not account creation

✅ The founder's testimony is visible and points to Christ

✅ The journey is introduced as a pathway of restoration, not a programme

✅ Partnership is secondary to the primary ministry invitation

✅ Every page and interaction uses language that keeps Jesus Christ at the centre

✅ The person can follow the full pathway from "I need help" to "I am being restored" to "I will help others"
