## The Four-Part Spine Applied to Each Element

**Every element should communicate at least one part of the spine:**

### Fraud is the Mission Field
- **Who should communicate this:** Hero section, founder story
- **Why:** The person needs to know this is specifically for them

### Jesus Christ is the Answer
- **Who should communicate this:** Hero section, founder story, journey intro, navigation
- **Why:** The person needs to know the answer is not a programme but Christ

### Restoration is the Journey
- **Who should communicate this:** Journey intro, navigation
- **Why:** The person needs to know what happens after freedom

### Testimony is the Multiplication
- **Who should communicate this:** Founder story, journey outcome
- **Why:** The person needs to see that transformed lives become witness

## Principle: Every Change Has a Reason

**Before making any redesign decision:**

❌ NOT: "This would look better"
✅ YES: "This change ensures someone desperate for freedom immediately recognizes we exist specifically for them and that Jesus Christ is our answer"

**Before changing any message:**

❌ NOT: "This sounds more professional"
✅ YES: "This change removes accidental corporate language that makes us sound like a platform instead of a ministry"

**Before moving any element:**

❌ NOT: "Better user experience"
✅ YES: "This positioning ensures the primary encounter with Jesus Christ's offer is not competed with by secondary CTAs"
