## The Audit Begins

**Status:** Ready to inspect actual homepage and fill in current messages.

**What needs to be examined:**

### 1. Hero Section
**Existing Element:** Homepage hero heading and supporting text

**Current Message:** "A Christian Community for Restoration" + "Restoration begins where deception ends" + narrative about reconciliation with God
**Desired Message:** Deliverance through Jesus Christ for those trapped in fraud (specific mission field named upfront)
**Reason for Change:** Someone in crisis needs to immediately recognize this ministry exists specifically for people trapped in fraud/deception. Current message sounds like generic Christian community, not specialized deliverance ministry
**Change Needed:** ✅ CHANGE — Eyebrow should name fraud/deception specifically; headline should emphasize deliverance through Christ

---

### 2. Primary CTA (Call to Action Button)
**Existing Element:** Main action button on homepage

**Current Message:** "Begin the Journey" (current) or similar platform-framing language
**Desired Message:** "I Need Deliverance"
**Reason for Change:** Four-Part Spine Test — **Jesus Christ is the Answer**. The person must see immediately that this is about freedom from fraud bondage through Christ, not about joining a programme. The button should match what someone actually trapped is thinking: "I need freedom from this life." Current language ("Begin the Journey") invites participation in a process. Desired language invites confession and encounter with Christ.
**Change Needed:** ✅ CHANGE — Replace primary CTA with language that reflects recognition of bondage and plea for deliverance, not programme participation

---

### 3. Founder's Testimony/Story
**Existing Element:** Homepage sections (not currently present as a dedicated section)

**Current Message:** ❌ MISSING — No founder testimony visible on homepage. No section that says "I was once trapped like you." 
**Desired Message:** "I was once trapped like you. Jesus Christ delivered me completely. Now I help others receive their deliverance from Him."
**Reason for Change:** Four-Part Spine Test — **Testimony is the Multiplication**. The person arriving must see proof that this is possible before committing to anything. The founder's presence should communicate: "Someone has walked this exact road and found freedom through Jesus Christ." It must be visible early (not hidden in About page) and must point to Christ's work, not personal achievement.
**Change Needed:** ✅ CRITICAL — Add founder's testimony section prominently (second or third element on homepage). Position it between hero CTA and journey introduction.

---

### 4. Journey Introduction
**Existing Element:** "What Happens Next" section (currently positioned 3rd on homepage: "You begin" → "You grow" → "You lead")

**Current Message:** ❌ WRONG ORDER — "You begin: Join our community. You grow: Follow seven stages. You lead: Guide others." Positioned as element #3, BEFORE deliverance encounter is clearly established. Frames journey as personal-improvement programme: "Here's what you'll do."
**Desired Message:** "After you encounter Jesus Christ's deliverance, you walk a restoration journey: Truth | Confession | Repentance | Forgiveness | Reconciliation | Honest Work | Serving." Positioned AFTER founder's testimony and primary deliverance CTA.
**Reason for Change:** Four-Part Spine Test — **Restoration is the Journey** (but only after deliverance through Christ). Current ordering accidentally frames this as self-improvement, not post-deliverance restoration. Order must be: (1) Deliverance encounter (Christ's work), (2) Founder's testimony (proof it's possible), (3) Then journey introduction (what happens next).
**Change Needed:** ✅ CRITICAL — Reorder homepage: move journey introduction to position after founder's testimony. Reframe messaging: "After deliverance, here's how restoration unfolds..." not "Here's what you do."

---

### 5. Navigation Structure
**Existing Element:** Fixed top navigation with: Logo "Restoration Community" | About | Journey | Sign In | Begin Journey (links to /auth/register)

**Current Message:** ❌ PLATFORM-FOCUSED — Navigation treats this like an app (Sign In | Begin Journey/Register). No "I Need Deliverance" link. No founder story access. "Begin Journey" links to registration, not to confession/prayer.
**Desired Message:** Navigation reflects spiritual priority: Home | About the Ministry | I Need Deliverance | The Journey | Partnership. Focus on encountering Jesus Christ, not creating accounts.
**Reason for Change:** Four-Part Spine Test — **Jesus Christ is the Answer**. Navigation design communicates whether this is a spiritual ministry or a platform. Current nav treats navigation like SaaS (Sign In | Register). The person seeking deliverance should see "I Need Deliverance" as the primary call, not "Begin Journey" as a registration prompt. Every nav item should move someone toward encounter with Christ.
**Change Needed:** ✅ CHANGE — Restructure navigation: remove platform language (Sign In, Begin Journey as registration). Add: "I Need Deliverance" as primary link. Reframe "Begin Journey" as journey explanation, not registration.

---

### 6. Partnership Invitation
**Existing Element:** No partnership section visible on current homepage

**Current Message:** ✅ CURRENTLY ALIGNED — Partnership is not competing for attention. No distracting fundraising or "partner with us" messaging on homepage. This is correct.
**Desired Message:** If/when partnership section is added: Small, secondary, Christ-focused: "This ministry exists because people who believe in this mission choose to stand with us. If you would like to partner with what Jesus Christ is doing through this work, send us a message."
**Reason for Change:** Four-Part Spine Test — **Testimony is the Multiplication**. Partnership invitation serves growth, but is NOT the primary ask. The person arriving is seeking freedom, not being invited to fund something. If partnership competes for attention, it accidentally makes the ministry about building, not freeing.
**Change Needed:** ✅ NO CHANGE NEEDED — Keep partnership hidden from primary journey. Add only when person is deep into journey or as footer element.

---

### 7. Tone and Visual Language
**Existing Element:** Overall design, typography, imagery, colour, messaging patterns across all homepage sections

**Current Message:** ⚠️ PARTIALLY MISALIGNED — Clean, professional design (good). Calm, spacious layout (good). But messaging consistently frames this as "Join community for restoration" and "Here's a programme you'll follow." Typography and spacing are excellent, but words say "platform" not "ministry."
**Desired Message:** Hope without denial | Specificity without judgment | Invitation without pressure | Jesus Christ unmistakably at centre | Doorway feeling (not platform)
**Reason for Change:** Four-Part Spine Test — **All Four Parts at once**. Visual design is good. Messaging tone is the issue. Person should feel: "This was built for people exactly like me. Jesus Christ can deliver me from this. Someone has walked this road and found freedom. I'm invited to begin an encounter with Christ, not sign up for a programme."
**Change Needed:** ✅ CHANGE (messaging focus) — Audit every paragraph/section for: (1) Does it mention Jesus Christ clearly? (2) Does it point to deliverance or to self-improvement? (3) Does it invite confession or platform participation? Current copy often gets 1-2 of 3 right, but needs all three.

## Critical Changes Identified (In Order of Implementation)

### Priority 1: Primary CTA
**Change:** "Begin the Journey" → "I Need Deliverance"
**Why:** Four-Part Spine: **Jesus Christ is the Answer**. Must reflect what someone trapped is actually thinking/feeling.
**Impact:** Highest leverage change. Shifts entire encounter from "join platform" to "confess need."

### Priority 2: Add Founder's Testimony Section
**Change:** Create new homepage section with founder's story
**Position:** After hero + CTA, before journey introduction
**Message:** "I was once trapped like you. Jesus Christ delivered me completely. Now I help others receive their deliverance from Him."
**Why:** Four-Part Spine: **Testimony is the Multiplication**. Proof that deliverance is real.

### Priority 3: Reorder Homepage Sections
**Change:** Move "What Happens Next" to position after founder's testimony
**Current order:** Hero → Why We Exist → What Happens Next → Final Invitation
**New order:** Hero → I Need Deliverance CTA → Founder Testimony → What Happens Next (reframed as "After Deliverance") → Final Invitation
**Why:** Four-Part Spine: **Restoration is the Journey** (but only after deliverance). Current order suggests journey is the path; new order shows Jesus is the path, then journey follows.

### Priority 4: Reframe Final Invitation
**Change:** Remove registration focus. Reframe as "Next Step: Confession/Prayer"
**Current:** "Ready to Begin? Join community and introduce yourself. [Begin Your Journey → /auth/register]"
**New:** "Ready to confess? Take the first step toward deliverance. [I Need Deliverance → /prayer-request or similar]"
**Why:** Four-Part Spine: **Fraud is the Mission Field**. First action must be confession, not account creation.

### Priority 5: Update Navigation
**Change:** Restructure nav to reflect spiritual priority
**Current:** About | Journey | Sign In | Begin Journey
**New:** Home | About the Ministry | I Need Deliverance | The Journey | Partner
**Why:** Four-Part Spine: **Jesus Christ is the Answer**. Nav should guide toward encountering Christ, not signing up.

### Priority 6: Audit Messaging Across All Sections
**Change:** Review each paragraph for three things:
1. Does it mention Jesus Christ clearly?
2. Does it point to deliverance (Christ's work) or self-improvement?
3. Does it invite confession or platform participation?
**Example issue:** "We exist because no one should have to rebuild their life alone" → Good sentiment, but doesn't say "Jesus Christ delivers." Better: "We exist because Jesus Christ alone can deliver people trapped in fraud and deception."
**Why:** Four-Part Spine: **All Four Parts**. Every message should reinforce the same truth.
