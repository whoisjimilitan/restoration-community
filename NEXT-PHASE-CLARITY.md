## The Four-Part Spine as Universal Test

Every document, page, and workflow should now test against:

### 1. Fraud is the Mission Field
**The test:** Does this clearly communicate the specific assignment?

❌ Too broad:
"We help people find purpose."

✅ Specific:
"We help people trapped in fraud and dishonest lifestyles find freedom through Jesus Christ."

**What this means:**
- The homepage must communicate this specificity
- Entry forms must ask about fraud/deception involvement
- Journey content must use fraud-specific language and examples
- Onboarding must address someone escaping a fraudulent life

### 2. Jesus Christ is the Answer
**The test:** Could this accidentally become education, coaching, or rehab?

❌ Wrong order:
Fraud → education → better choices → restoration

✅ Correct order:
Fraud bondage → encounter with Jesus Christ → deliverance → restoration → honest living

**What this means:**
- The first message must be about deliverance, not improvement
- The CTA should be "I need deliverance" not "I need prayer"
- Journey stages must point back to Jesus, not self-effort
- Testimony must remain "Jesus delivered me," not "I fixed myself"

### 3. Restoration is the Journey
**The test:** Does each stage clearly follow deliverance and prepare for testimony?

**Stage 1: Truth**
Does it include: "I must acknowledge where I am and what I have become"?

**Stage 2: Confession**
Does it connect confession before God with freedom?

**Stage 6: Honest Work** (The Critical One)
Does it communicate: "new identity produces truthful living"?
NOT: "Get a job instead of scamming"
BUT: "A person transformed by Christ lives according to truth, integrity, responsibility, and God's purpose"

**Stage 7: Serving**
Does it communicate: "the person once trapped becomes a witness to others"?

### 4. Testimony is Multiplication
**The test:** Is the story pointing to Jesus or to achievement?

❌ Wrong:
"Look at what Brother Jimi achieved."

✅ Right:
"I was once trapped like you. Jesus Christ delivered me completely. Now I help others receive their deliverance from Him."

**What this means:**
- Your testimony is the doorway that says "This is possible"
- But the hero remains Jesus Christ
- Testimony should be central in public identity
- Founder story should be visible everywhere

## The Most Important Remaining Issue: Public Expression

Claude identified this accurately:
"Public experience may prioritize 'community' over 'deliverance.'"

**Why this matters:**

Someone arriving at the ministry who is trapped in fraud is probably not searching for:
- Community
- Fellowship
- A programme
- A seven-stage journey

They are searching for:
- **How do I get out?**
- **Is there freedom from this?**
- **Can I change?**

**The first message must meet them there.**

### Current Risk
If the homepage communicates "Join a Christian community," but the person is thinking "I am trapped. I need freedom," there is a mismatch.

### What Needs to Happen
Audit the public expression:
- Homepage hero message
- CTA language (not "I need prayer" but "I need deliverance")
- Founder story visibility
- Onboarding tone and questions
- Journey introduction messaging

Each should communicate:
"You don't have to remain trapped in fraud. Jesus Christ can deliver and restore your life."

## The Name Question: Wait and Then Decide

Current: "Restoration Community"
Possible: "Restoration"

**Do not change yet.** Here's why:

The pathway is:
Deliverance → Restoration → Community → Service → Testimony

"Community" describes what happens later in the journey.
"Restoration" describes what God is doing.

So the question is: Does the name describe the first encounter or the final outcome?

**Process:**
1. Complete the public identity audit (homepage, CTA, founder story, onboarding)
2. Ask: "Does the name still fit the first encounter?"
3. Then decide: Keep "Restoration Community" or move to "Restoration"?

Changing the name before the expression is clear puts the cart before the horse.
