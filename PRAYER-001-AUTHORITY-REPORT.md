# PRAYER-001 Authority Report: Prayer in the Restoration Community Platform

## Executive Summary

**Prayer governance is extensive and deep.** Books One, Two, and Three are clear about what prayer IS and WHY it matters.

**Prayer mechanics are undefined.** The governance does NOT specify HOW the platform should enable prayer.

This report identifies the theological foundation and the implementation gap.

---

## Part One: What the Governance Says Prayer IS

### Core Principle: Prayer is Community Identity, Not a Feature

**Book One (Lines 633-651):**
> "### We Pray Together
>
> Prayer reminds us that restoration is ultimately God's work. We pray for wisdom. We pray for courage. We pray for honest work. We pray for families. We pray for strength during difficult seasons. We pray for one another faithfully.
>
> **Prayer is not an activity added to our community. It is part of the life we share together.**"

**Book Three (Lines 695-710):**
> "## A Praying Community
>
> **Prayer is not an activity added to the community. Prayer is part of who we are.** We pray with one another. We pray for one another. We thank God together. We bring our burdens before Him together.
>
> **The platform should encourage a culture where prayer is natural, regular, and sincere.** Every participant should know that they are supported not only by people, but also by prayer."

**Critical Insight:** Prayer is not a feature to bolt on. It's woven into the community fabric. The platform should make it "natural, regular, and sincere."

---

### Prayer's Purpose: God's Work, Not Human Effort

**Book Two (Line 1093):**
> "Every believer can pray."

**Book Three (Line 458):**
> "Prayer reminds both mentor and participant that restoration ultimately belongs to God."

Prayer is acknowledgment that restoration is God's work, not human achievement.

---

### Prayer in Mentoring Relationships

**Book One, Part Six (Lines 1309-1324):**
> "### Praying Together
>
> Mentors pray faithfully for those they walk alongside. They pray for wisdom. They pray for courage. They pray for honest work. They pray for restored relationships. They pray that Christ will continue His work of restoration in every life.
>
> Prayer reminds both mentor and participant that lasting transformation belongs to God."

**Book Three, Chapter Three (Lines 448-458):**
> "Prayer remains central to every mentoring relationship. Mentors pray with participants. Participants pray for one another.
>
> **The platform should make prayer a natural and regular part of communication.** Requests should be treated with wisdom, respect, and appropriate confidentiality.
>
> Prayer reminds both mentor and participant that restoration ultimately belongs to God."

**Key Details:**
- Mentors pray FOR participants
- Mentors pray WITH participants
- Prayer is part of communication rhythm
- Prayer requests require "wisdom, respect, and appropriate confidentiality"

## Part Two: What Governance Does NOT Specify

### Missing Implementation Details

The governance is clear on WHAT and WHY but silent on HOW.

**Undefined:**

1. **Prayer Request Mechanism**
   - Can participants post prayer requests?
   - To whom? (Mentors only? Whole community?)
   - Public or private?
   - How are requests managed/stored?

2. **Prayer Response Mechanism**
   - How do people pray for a request?
   - Do they log it?
   - Do they notify the requestor?
   - Is prayer private or can it be shared?

3. **Prayer Categories or Types**
   - Should prayer requests be organized?
   - By personal area? (family, work, temptation, etc.)
   - By urgency?
   - By confidentiality level?

4. **Prayer History & Accountability**
   - Are prayers recorded?
   - Kept private?
   - Reviewed for faithfulness?
   - How long are requests kept?

5. **Mentor Role in Prayer**
   - Do mentors initiate prayer requests?
   - Do mentors coordinate group prayer?
   - Can mentors see all prayer requests?
   - Is there mentor accountability for praying?

6. **Community Prayer vs. Personal Prayer**
   - Is prayer individual?
   - Paired (mentor-participant)?
   - Group-based?
   - All of the above?

7. **Integration with Journey Stages**
   - Should prayer be linked to reflection?
   - To stage progression?
   - Tied to specific struggles?
   - Or separate community practice?

8. **Prayer Scheduling/Rhythms**
   - "Regular" and "natural" — but what does that look like?
   - Daily reminders?
   - Weekly prayer focuses?
   - Tied to gatherings?

## Part Three: What Governance Requires Must Be True

### Non-Negotiable Principles

**Whatever implementation is chosen must preserve:**

1. **Prayer as Community Identity**
   - Not a feature added to the platform
   - Woven into natural community rhythm
   - Makes prayer feel normal, not obligatory

2. **Confidentiality & Wisdom**
   - Prayer requests "treated with wisdom, respect, and appropriate confidentiality"
   - Not public by default
   - Appropriate access controls

3. **God's Work, Not Human Achievement**
   - Prayer reminds that restoration is God's work
   - Not tracking "prayer metrics" or scoring prayers
   - Focuses on faith, not compliance

4. **Naturalness & Regularity**
   - "Natural, regular, and sincere"
   - Not gamified or forced
   - Woven into communication patterns

5. **Between-Gathering Connection**
   - Helps people care for each other when not in person
   - "We are supported not only by people, but also by prayer"
   - Technology enables connection

6. **Mentoring Centerpiece**
   - Prayer is central to mentor-participant relationship
   - Mentors pray for and with participants
   - Signals that mentors are spiritually invested

## Part Five: Dependency Analysis

### Why Prayer Comes Now (in sequence)

**Ministry Dependencies:**
```
Participant Identity (AUTH-001, ONBOARD-001) ✅
    ↓
Participant Journey (JOURNEY-001, JOURNEY-002) ✅
    ↓
Community Support (PRAYER-001) ← YOU ARE HERE
    ↓
Mentoring Relationships (MENTORING-001)
    ↓
Group Gatherings (COMMUNITY-001)
```

Prayer is foundational to community support before formal mentoring and group structures exist.

**Why Not Later:**
- Prayer is "part of who we are" — not secondary
- Mentors need to pray for participants — should be early
- Community needs prayer language before gatherings form
- Reflection (JOURNEY-002) ✅ naturally leads to prayer

## Summary: What Ministry Must Decide

Before PRAYER-001 can be implemented, Ministry Authority must choose:

1. **What triggers a prayer request?**
   - Participant initiates?
   - Mentor initiates?
   - Both?

2. **Who can pray?**
   - Assigned mentor only?
   - Whole community?
   - Specific prayer partners?

3. **How is prayer logged?**
   - Recorded in database?
   - Private between two people?
   - Visible to requester only?

4. **What's the scope?**
   - Just mentor-participant prayer?
   - Community prayer too?
   - Prayer requests channel?
   - All of the above?

5. **How does this relate to gatherings?**
   - Separate from in-person prayer?
   - Continuous between gatherings?
   - Preparation for group gatherings?

6. **Is prayer linked to journey or separate?**
   - Prayer requests tied to stages?
   - Prayer independent of journey?
   - Both?
