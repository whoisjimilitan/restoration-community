### **LIGHTBULB 2: 7-DAY > 7-WEEK**

**Weekly model problems:**
- Participants drop out mid-journey
- Hard to maintain emotional intensity
- Takes 2 months for proof
- Families/work conflicts arise
- Facilitators burn out mentoring

**7-day intensive advantages:**
- **Immersive:** People experience full transformation arc in one week
- **Memory:** Intensive experiences stick (neuroscience ✓)
- **Bonding:** Close relationships form = lifelong co-facilitators
- **Content:** One location, one week = continuous recording opportunity
- **Replicability:** "7-day program" is easy to scale to 10 cities
- **Testimonies:** "My life changed in 7 days" is powerful story
- **Scheduling:** Works for employed people (take a week off)
- **Cost:** 7 days in one location < 7 weeks travel + accommodation

**Example iteration:**
```
WEEK 1 (7-day intensive): Cohort 1 at Location A (Accra, Kumasi, etc.)
- Monday: Stage 1 (Truth)
- Tuesday: Stage 2 (Confession)
- Wednesday: Stage 3 (Repentance)
- Thursday: Stage 4 (Forgiveness)
- Friday: Stage 5 (Reconciliation)
- Saturday: Stage 6 (Honest Work)
- Sunday: Stage 7 (Service) + Facilitator commitment

EACH DAY:
- Morning: Teaching (recorded)
- Afternoon: Reflection + Prayer
- Evening: Testimony + bonding

WEEK 2: Break (participants reflect, Brother Jimi edits/uploads videos)

WEEK 3: Cohort 2 at Location B (Different city)
- Same 7-day intensive
- 2-3 people from Cohort 1 co-facilitate (proof of transformation)
- New participants see facilitators = "This works"

WEEK 5: Cohort 3 at Location C
- 4-5 people from Cohorts 1-2 co-facilitate
- Expanding network of trained leaders
```

**By Month 3:**
- 3 completed cohorts = 45 transformed people
- 12-15 trained facilitators
- 21 video episodes (7 stages × 3 cohorts)
- 100+ YouTube subscribers
- Multiple institutional pilots

**Design implication:** Dashboard tracks VIDEO PRODUCTION and FACILITATOR PIPELINE, not weekly attendance.
