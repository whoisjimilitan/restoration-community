If you haven’t yet

Receive Jesus.

  1. God loves you.

    “For God so loved the world that he gave his one and only Son…”John 3:16

  2. Sin keeps you from Him.

    “For all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God.”Romans 3:23

  3. Jesus paid the price.

    “While we were still sinners, Christ died for us.”Romans 5:8

  4. Receive Him by faith.

    “Everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved.”Romans 10:13

If you’re ready, pray this

Jesus, I believe You died for my sinsand rose again.I turn from my sinand receive You as my Lord and Saviour.Thank You for loving me.Amen.
