Receive Jesus.

It’s the most important decision you’ll ever make.

  1. God loves you.

    “For God so loved the world that he gave his one and only Son, that whoever believes in him shall not perish but have eternal life.”

  2. But sin keeps you from Him.

    “For all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God.”

  3. So Jesus paid the price for your sin

    by His death on the cross, and rose again.

    “…Christ died for our sins according to the Scriptures, that he was buried, that he was raised on the third day according to the Scriptures.”

  4. Now receive Him by faith.

    “Yet to all who did receive him, to those who believed in his name, he gave the right to become children of God.”

If you believe this, pray this with me

Jesus, I believe You died for my sinsand rose again.I turn from my sinand receive You as my Lord and Saviour.Thank You for loving me.Amen.

Did you pray this today?

I’d love to rejoice with you, and walk with you for 90 mornings.
