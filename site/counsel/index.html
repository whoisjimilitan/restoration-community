There is value in challenges

You see that situation that looks impossible? I assure you there is value in it.

Anything close to Jesus receives attack. Your challenge may be sickness or poverty. And the devil will ask where your God is.

But when the sea is calm, no one is really tested. It is only when challenges come that we know whether truly you are a Christian. That is how the devil gets many to his side: they measure God’s presence by their circumstances.

“Consider it pure joy, my brothers and sisters, whenever you face trials of many kinds, because you know that the testing of your faith produces perseverance.”James 1:2-3

Your challenge is meant to improve you, not to impair you.

So do not listen to the panic of the moment. Watch and pray. Be still, and hear what the Holy Spirit is saying. God has not abandoned you.

True believers welcome adversity as a friend. Match your actions with your words, for action is of the heart, and words are of the mouth.

Today, say: Lord, release my spirit to follow You. I have surrendered everything to You. Give me the grace to overcome my pain, in Jesus’ name. Amen.
