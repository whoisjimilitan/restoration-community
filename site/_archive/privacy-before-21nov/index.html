Privacy

Your trust matters.

What I keep, why, and how to have it removed.

What I keep

Counsel is delivered through WhatsApp Channel. I never collect your number. When you follow the Channel, WhatsApp keeps your number private between you and me—I don’t see it or store it.

Anything you send me: a prayer request, a reply, or a phone number so I can call you. Only I read these. They are never shown on this site and never shared.

If you choose to partner, Stripe handles your payment. I never see or keep your card details.

What I don’t do

I never sell, rent or share your details. There are no adverts and no tracking scripts on this site.

Where it’s kept

Counsel is posted to the Brother Jimi WhatsApp Channel. Prayer requests and phone numbers are kept privately in Notion. Payments go through Stripe.

Leaving, or having it removed

You can leave the WhatsApp Channel any time. To have anything you’ve sent me deleted, write to jimi@brotherjimi.com and it will be removed.
