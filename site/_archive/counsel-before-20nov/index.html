Before you check your phone, open your Bible. Take in one verse and say it out loud.

Joshua 1:8

When something goes wrong, whose voice do you hear first? It's usually the one you've been listening to the most.

If you've filled your mind with fear and noise, that's what rises up. But if you've been taking in God's Word day after day, you'll hear Him first.

So start today. Before anything else, open your Bible. One verse. Say it out loud. Tonight, meditate on it again.

What you hear first tomorrow depends on what you listen to today.
