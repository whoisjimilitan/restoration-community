Privacy

What I keep, why and how to have it removed.

What I keep

Counsel comes through my WhatsApp Channel. I never see your number. WhatsApp keeps it private.

When you register for a City Encounter, the page opens WhatsApp with a message you send yourself. Your name, region and number arrive as that message. Only I and the host team read it.

Anything else you send me, a prayer request, a reply or a number for a call, is read only by me. It is never published or shared.

What I never do

I never sell, rent or share your details. This site has no adverts and no tracking.

Leaving, or having it removed

You can leave the Channel at any time. To have anything you sent me deleted, write to jimi@brotherjimi.com.
