Privacy

What I keep, why, and how to have it removed.

  1. Keep

    Only what you send me

    When you register for a City Encounter, WhatsApp opens with a message you send yourself. Your name, region and number arrive in that message. Only I and the host team read it.

    The daily counsel is free to read here and on my WhatsApp Channel. On the Channel, I never see your number.

    Anything else you send me, a prayer request or a reply, is read only by me.

  2. Never

    No selling, no tracking

    I never sell, rent or share your details. This site has no adverts and no tracking.

    Your phone remembers one small thing for you: that you have sent a letter to your pastor, so you are not asked again. It stays on your phone.

  3. Remove

    Gone when you ask

    Leave the Channel at any time. To have anything you sent me deleted, write to hello@brotherjimi.com.
