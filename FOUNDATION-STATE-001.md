# FOUNDATION-STATE-001
## Restoration Community Frozen Foundation Reference

**Purpose:** Quick orientation for future contributors about what is permanent and immovable.

**Not an authority document. A map.**

---

## Frozen Foundational Documents

As of **2026-07-25**, the following documents are frozen and form the permanent foundation of the Restoration Community platform:

### Constitutional Layer (Changes only if ministry fundamentally changes)

**MISSION-001: Restoration Ministry Foundation**
- Why we exist: Jesus Christ alone restores hearts
- What we believe: Prayer is gateway, testimony is fruit, community supports
- Identity: We are a prayer ministry, not a social platform
- Status: Frozen 2026-07-25

**ARCHITECTURE-001: Authority Flow and Architectural Design**
- How authority flows: Jesus Christ → Scripture → Mission → Governance → Operations → Requirements → Implementation → Interface
- Permanent principle: Platform is inside ministry, not ministry inside platform
- Direction: Authority flows downward, never upward
- Governance stability: Authorities stabilize, not perpetually grow
- Status: Frozen 2026-07-25

### Foundational Governance Layer (Changes only if ministry practice fundamentally changes)

**GOV-002: Restoration Prayer Ministry Principle**
- What prayer is: Spiritual foundation, first act of welcome, accompanies restoration
- What prayer is not: Feature, metric, game, social activity
- Who leads: Jesus Christ as source; Brother Jimi as pastoral leader
- Protection: Confidentiality is enforced; vulnerability is pastoral
- Status: Frozen 2026-07-25

**ENTRY-001: Restoration Ministry Entry Pathway**
- How seekers discover ministry: Prayer is the entry point
- Relationship precedes registration
- Ministry responds personally (not automated)
- Personal encounter leads to decision
- Status: Frozen 2026-07-25

---

## The Immovable Principle

**The platform is inside the ministry, not the ministry inside the platform.**

This principle explains why:
- Prayer is the doorway, not a feature
- Registration is not the first interaction
- Community supports restoration, not replaces Jesus Christ
- Brother Jimi's role is pastoral, not administrative
- Metrics and gamification are incompatible with spiritual transformation
- Testimony is fruit of God's work, not a social mechanism
- Confidentiality is non-negotiable

---

## The Authority Direction

```
MISSION-001
    ↓ (Why we exist)
ARCHITECTURE-001
    ↓ (How authority flows)
GOV-002 & ENTRY-001
    ↓ (How ministry operates)
EXECUTABLES
    ↓ (What platform must support)
IMPLEMENTATION
    ↓ (Code that serves ministry)
VERIFICATION
    ↓ (Proof of faithful implementation)
```

Authority flows **downward only**. No layer above can be redefined by layers below.

---

## Rule for Future Contributors

**No future executable or governance document may contradict these foundations.**

If a new question arises:
1. Check MISSION-001 first (is this about ministry identity?)
2. Check ARCHITECTURE-001 second (does this respect authority flow?)
3. Check GOV-002 and ENTRY-001 third (does this align with established practice?)
4. Only then create new governance or executables within this framework

---

## What This Foundation Prevents

- ❌ Treating the platform as the hero of restoration
- ❌ Building prayer as a social feature or game
- ❌ Letting software redefine what ministry is
- ❌ Replacing pastoral authority with automation
- ❌ Measuring success by engagement metrics
- ❌ Making registration the first interaction
- ❌ Letting interface design decisions define the ministry

---

## What This Foundation Enables

- ✅ Clear pastoral authority
- ✅ Stable long-term ministry partnership
- ✅ Software that serves, not redefines
- ✅ Confident governance and executable development
- ✅ New contributors who understand what is immovable
- ✅ Growth that doesn't require re-discovering foundational questions

---

## The Next Phase

Development now proceeds to define how the ministry responds when someone in desperation requests prayer.

**ENCOUNTER-001** will answer: "What does faithful ministry response look like?"

Not software questions yet. Pastoral questions first.

Only after the ministry encounter is defined should software workflows be designed.

---

## Reference for Builders

When beginning a new feature or governance decision, ask:

1. "Does this serve the frozen foundation?"
2. "Which frozen foundation principle does this depend on?"
3. "Could I explain this to someone by reference only to these four documents?"

If you cannot answer these questions cleanly, the work is not yet ready.
