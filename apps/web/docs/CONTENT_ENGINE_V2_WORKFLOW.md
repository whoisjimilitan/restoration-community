# Content Engine v2 Workflow Guide

## Overview

The Identity-Centered Content Engine v2 produces 9 authentic content formats from one piece of raw material (article, podcast, observation, etc.). The engine applies a 5-part framework that maintains Brother Jimi's authentic voice while varying how each piece frames the message.

Think of it like this: You have one core truth. The engine turns it into 9 different conversations about that same truth, each one tailored to a different format and audience. All of them sound like Brother Jimi talking to one friend.

---

## The 5-Part Framework

Every piece of content produced by the engine follows this structure. This is what makes it sound authentic.

### 1. Conversational Entry
**What it is:** A natural opening line, like how Brother Jimi would start a conversation with one friend.

**Examples (different frames):**
- Counsel frame: "I see what's happening here"
- Advise frame: "You know what your real problem is?"
- Uplift frame: "You're stronger than you think"
- Enlighten frame: "This is what people miss"
- Educate frame: "Let me break this down for you"

**Why it matters:** It sets the tone and makes the reader feel like they're having a real conversation, not reading marketing copy. No corporate jargon. No sales pitch. Just one person talking to another.

### 2. Frame Intention
**What it is:** The angle or lens through which the content is presented. Not a tone change—the voice stays bold and raw—but a different intention.

**The 5 Frames:**

| Frame | Intention | Example |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| **Counsel** | Observational, diagnostic | "Here's what I see happening in your life" |
| **Advise** | Direct, prescriptive | "Here's what you need to do differently" |
| **Uplift** | Empowering, strengthening | "Here's why you're capable of this" |
| **Enlighten** | Revelatory, truth-revealing | "Here's what's actually real" |
| **Educate** | Teaching, instructive | "Here's how this works" |

**How frames work:**
- Same material with Counsel frame → Diagnostic observation
- Same material with Advise frame → Specific direction
- Same material with Uplift frame → Empowerment
- Same material with Enlighten frame → Truth revelation
- Same material with Educate frame → Understanding

The frame is determined by a rotation algorithm: `(identityChoice + contentIndex) % 5`. This means:
- Same material generates 5 different pieces by rotating through frames
- Frame rotation is predictable and consistent
- Brother Jimi can rely on frame order when planning

### 3. Bold Voice
**What it is:** Brother Jimi's authentic voice—raw, prophetic, direct, unpolished.

**Characteristics:**
- Short sentences, staccato phrasing (not flowing prose)
- Contractions: "I'm", "don't", "you've"
- Direct address: "You", "I", "Listen"
- No corporate jargon, flowery language, or formal tone
- Conversational, like thinking out loud
- Trusts the reader to connect the dots

**Example (bad, sounds corporate):**
"This profound revelation will abundantly transform your perspective on life's journey."

**Example (good, sounds like Brother Jimi):**
"You see this? This changes everything."

### 4. Natural Cadence
**What it is:** How the language flows—thinking out loud, not delivering a speech.

**Characteristics:**
- Thoughts build naturally, not linearly
- Asks questions to engage the reader
- Uses examples, stories, lived experience
- No formal transitions or connectors ("Furthermore," "In conclusion,")
- Feels like someone talking, not writing
- Pauses and emphases come through in short sentences

### 5. Weight
**What it is:** Every word earns its place. No filler, no padding, no unnecessary adjectives.

**Characteristics:**
- Each word serves a purpose
- Adjectives are functional, not decorative
- No repeated ideas unless emphasizing
- Concise but not rushed
- "Show, don't tell"

## How to Use the Engine

### Step 1: Provide Raw Material
Give the engine one piece of material:
- WordPress blog post (excerpt or full)
- Podcast transcript (key section, 2-3 paragraphs)
- Observation or revelation (thoughts from the day)
- Scripture reflection (personal insights)
- Testimony snippet (transformation story)

**Keep it under 500 words for best results.** The engine will distill it to its essence.

### Step 2: Engine Extracts Meaning
The engine automatically:
1. **Identifies the identity choice** (1-7) the material centers on
2. **Extracts the voice theme** (revelation, contrast, examples, call-to-identity)
3. **Selects a frame** based on content index

This happens behind the scenes. No manual work required.

### Step 3: Engine Generates 9 Formats
The engine produces:

| Format | Length | Use Case |
|--------|--------|----------|
| **Daily Letter** | 2-3 paragraphs | Newsletter, personal reflection |
| **Social Post** | <280 chars | Twitter, LinkedIn |
| **Micro Insight** | 1-2 sentences | Quote graphic, snapshot |
| **Devotional** | 3-4 sentences | Morning reflection, email |
| **Article** | 500-800 words | Blog post, long-form thought |
| **Short Video** | 30-60 sec script | YouTube Short, Reel, TikTok |
| **Long Video** | 5-10 min script | YouTube, teaching video |
| **Podcast Moment** | 90-120 sec script | Podcast clip, audio snippet |
| **Email** | Full newsletter | Email campaign, subscriber message |

Each format follows the same 5-part framework but is optimized for its medium.

### Step 4: Brother Jimi Refines (Optional)
The engine produces 95% quality. Brother Jimi can do final refinement:
- Adjusts cadence if anything feels off
- Adds personal touches or specific examples
- Fixes any phrasing that doesn't feel natural
- Adds Scripture or specific local references
- Sharpens emphasis or removes anything that feels forced

**This refinement is NOT rewriting—it's polishing 95% to 100%.**

## Quality Assurance

### Voice Validation System
Every piece is automatically validated against:
- ✅ **Authentic markers** → contractions, direct address, conversational tone
- ❌ **Anti-patterns** → flowery language, corporate jargon, soft language, formal phrases
- 📊 **Confidence score** (0-1) → how authentic the piece feels

### QA Checklist Before Publishing
- [ ] Frame rotation is clear (different frames = visibly different angles)
- [ ] No corporate language or filler
- [ ] Conversational entry feels natural
- [ ] Every paragraph earns its place (no fluff)
- [ ] Voice is consistent across all 9 formats
- [ ] Examples feel lived/authentic
- [ ] Length constraints met (social post is short, article is long, etc.)
- [ ] No forbidden words (leverage, synergy, utilize, facilitate, etc.)

## Frame Rotation Strategy

### Predictable Frame Order
Using the same material multiple times:
- **Content Index 0** → Frame A
- **Content Index 1** → Frame B (different)
- **Content Index 2** → Frame C (different)
- **Content Index 3** → Frame D (different)
- **Content Index 4** → Frame E (different)
- **Content Index 5** → Frame A (repeats)

### Why This Matters
- You can plan content calendar knowing which frame comes next
- Readers see different angles on the same truth
- One piece of material becomes a multi-week content strategy
- No frame repeats until you've hit all 5

### Example: One Material → 5 Pieces
From one revelation about "choosing your identity":

1. **Index 0 (Counsel):** "Here's what I see in your choices"
   - Diagnostic observation
   - "Here's what's happening in how you're building your identity"

2. **Index 1 (Advise):** "Here's what you need to do differently"
   - Prescriptive direction
   - "Here's what you need to understand about how choices shape you"

3. **Index 2 (Uplift):** "Here's why you're capable of this"
   - Empowerment
   - "You're stronger than you think"

4. **Index 3 (Enlighten):** "Here's the truth nobody's saying"
   - Truth revelation
   - "This is what people miss about identity"

5. **Index 4 (Educate):** "Here's how identity actually works"
   - Understanding/teaching
   - "Let me break this down for you"

Same material, 5 completely different angles.

## Voice Guidelines

### DO:
- Use short sentences
- Start with conversational entries that sound natural
- Ask questions to engage the reader
- Use contractions (I'm, don't, you've)
- Be direct and bold
- Speak from lived experience
- Let examples do the work
- Trust the reader to understand
- Use active voice
- Be specific (not vague)

### DON'T:
- Use flowery adjectives ("beautiful," "profound," "amazing")
- Sound like a corporate memo
- Hedge or soften language ("perhaps," "maybe," "it could be argued")
- Write formal sentences ("Furthermore," "In conclusion,")
- Explain everything—let readers think
- Use corporate jargon (leverage, synergy, utilize, facilitate)
- Sound like you're preaching
- Use passive voice
- Be vague or wishy-washy
- Repeat the same idea three times

## Troubleshooting

### Content Feels Polished/Formal
**Problem:** Output sounds like corporate writing, not Brother Jimi.

**Solution:**
- Check voice guardrails output (warnings)
- Look for flowery adjectives or corporate language
- Increase contractions, shorten sentences
- Add more direct address ("You", "I")
- Remove qualifiers ("perhaps", "somewhat")

### Frame Feels Wrong
**Problem:** The frame doesn't match what you expected.

**Solution:**
- Confirm contentIndex is what you expect
- Verify identity extraction is correct
- Check if conversational entry matches the frame you wanted
- Try different contentIndex to rotate to next frame

### Output Doesn't Feel Authentic
**Problem:** It reads like AI-generated, not Brother Jimi.

**Solution:**
- Review confidence score (<0.7 = needs attention)
- Check guardrails warnings in detail
- Compare to Brother Jimi's actual voice samples
- Add more examples from his lived experience
- Simplify vocabulary and sentence structure

### Social Post Is Too Long
**Problem:** Post exceeds 280 characters.

**Solution:**
- Engine should handle this automatically
- If it doesn't, manually trim to first sentence + call to identity
- Keep revelation snippet under 100 chars
- Remove any unnecessary words

### Examples Don't Feel Real
**Problem:** Supporting examples sound generic or disconnected.

**Solution:**
- Provide more detailed raw material
- Include specific stories or experiences
- Examples should come directly from raw material
- They should feel like lived wisdom, not theory

- Daily Letter (newsletter)
- Social Post (awareness)
- Article (long-form)
- Angle: Diagnostic observation—"Here's what I see"

**Week 2 (Index 1, Advise):**
- Devotional (reflection)

**Week 3 (Index 2, Uplift):**
- Micro Insight (snapshot)
- Short Video (engagement)
- Social Post (inspiration)
- Angle: Empowerment—"Here's why you can"

**Week 4 (Index 3, Enlighten):**
- Article (deep dive)
- Long Video (teaching)

Generate 5 weeks of content (one full frame rotation) from each month's theme:
- Month 1: Identity (5 pieces × 9 formats = 45 pieces)

This gives you 3+ months of completely planned, frame-rotated content from just 3 raw materials.
