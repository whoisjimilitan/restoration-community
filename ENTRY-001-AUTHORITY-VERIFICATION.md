## Part 7: Critical Distinction This Preserves

### Why Separate Entry Pathway from Journey Pathway?

**PRAYER-001** (Authenticated Participant Prayer)
```
Participant in restoration journey
    ↓
Requests prayer support during journey
    ↓
Private prayer from ministry
    ↓
Strengthens journey walking
```

**ENTRY-001 + ENCOUNTER-001** (Unauthenticated Seeker Prayer)
```
Visitor/seeker outside system
    ↓
Requests prayer (no account)
    ↓
Ministry responds and encounters person
    ↓
Person enters journey (if they choose)
```

**Why this matters:**
- PRAYER-001 serves people already **inside** the system
- ENTRY-001 serves people still **outside** the system
- Both are necessary; they serve different needs
- Both honor the same principle (prayer + ministry-led + confidential)

**Without this distinction, the platform would likely:**
- Force seekers through account creation first
- Miss the mercy moment
- Treat entry like membership
- Undermine the mission
