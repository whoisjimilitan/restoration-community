# GOV-002
## Restoration Prayer Ministry Principle

This document governs the ministry's prayer practice. It derives from MISSION-001 and establishes the authoritative principles that PRAYER-001 and all future prayer-related executables must serve.

---

## Purpose

Prayer is not a feature of the platform.

Prayer is the ministry's first act of welcome for anyone seeking restoration.

Prayer is the spiritual foundation underneath the entire restoration journey.

When someone in desperation says "I need help," the ministry responds with prayer—connecting them to Jesus Christ and the pastoral care of trusted servants.

---

## Spiritual Principle

**Prayer accompanies restoration from entry through transformation.**

Prayer is how desperate people encounter Jesus Christ.

Prayer is how participants seek strength during the restoration journey.

Prayer is how the community carries one another.

Prayer is the channel through which God's transforming work is acknowledged and received.

Prayer is not:
- A feature to achieve or complete
- A metric to measure or track
- An activity to quantify or gamify
- A social experience to broadcast
- A substitute for pastoral care

Prayer is:
- A trusted doorway to God's mercy
- Led by pastoral authority
- Confidential and protected
- Personal and voluntary
- A work of the Holy Spirit in response to human need

---

## Ministry Leadership

**Jesus Christ is the source of restoration.**

Jesus Christ is the Saviour and the one who transforms hearts.

**Brother Jimi serves as pastor, teacher, mentor, and prayer leader** under Christ's authority through Scripture.

The prayer ministry operates under Brother Jimi's pastoral leadership and serves the restoration work of the Restoration Community.

The platform exists to support this ministry—never to replace it, never to automate it, never to remove the human pastoral element.

---

## Confidentiality and Care

Prayer requests are **private by default**.

Vulnerability belongs first to God and to trusted servants, not to community.

Those who pray requests are not required to disclose their struggles publicly or socially.

The ministry receives and holds prayer requests with pastoral wisdom.

Confidentiality is enforced at every level—technical, organizational, and relational.

---

## Testimony

Testimony is the natural fruit of God's transforming work.

Participants are encouraged toward testimony as an expression of gratitude for God's work and as a witness that invites others to seek restoration.

Testimony flows from genuine transformation, not from program completion or platform participation.

The ministry disciplines participants toward authentic testimony while respecting readiness and circumstances.

Testimony is never required, never gamified, never used to measure program success.

---

## Ministry Role

**The platform supports prayer ministry. The platform never replaces pastoral care.**

The platform provides:
- Connection: A means for seekers to reach the ministry
- Organization: Orderly management of prayer requests and responses
- Stewardship: Records, follow-up, and accountability

The platform does not provide:
- The prayer itself
- Spiritual discernment
- Pastoral authority
- Transformation (that's God's work)
- Substitute for human relationship

---

## Authority and Derivation

This principle is established in:
- **MISSION-001:** Prayer is the gateway to encounter with Jesus Christ
- **ARCHITECTURE-001:** Governance precedes implementation; the platform serves the ministry
- **Four-Book Foundation (Book Three):** The platform exists to serve ministry, not create it

This document governs:
- **PRAYER-001:** Prayer request system implementation
- Any future prayer-related executables or features
- Any ministry decisions about prayer practice

Future additions to prayer ministry practice must derive from this principle and remain consistent with it.

---

## Permanent Status

**GOV-002 is canonical governance.**

It establishes the foundational principle governing all prayer-related work.

It changes only if the ministry's understanding of prayer ministry fundamentally changes.

It is not a feature specification, implementation guide, or technical document.

It is the governing principle that all technical and operational decisions must serve.
